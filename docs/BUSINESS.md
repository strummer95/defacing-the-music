Saved Nov 24, 2026. Duck: "save this info for DnR but i have no reason to do this now... when i actually need a bank account." Nothing here is urgent until real money is coming in. Not legal or tax advice; confirm with whoever set up DnR.

- **No bank account** for DnR yet. No money coming in yet.

## When to do this
When the first real money is on the horizon: a sponsor saying yes, YouTube or Spotify monetization getting close, or merch. Until then, nothing to do. Small show purchases (domain, gear) just get noted somewhere as business expenses.

## The steps, in order
1. **EIN for DnR** (the LLC's tax ID). Free at irs.gov, about 10 minutes. DnR may already have one from when it was set up; check first.
2. **Business checking account for DnR.** Free online options with no monthly fee and no minimum balance (check the fine print when signing up, these change):
   - **Relay:** built for small businesses; lets you open several separate accounts under one business for free, so each DnR project (DTM and the other sites) can have its own. Claude's pick if DnR will have more than one project making money.
   - **Mercury:** the slickest app, very simple, one main account. Equally good if DTM is the only thing making money.
   - Also free: Bluevine, Found (aimed at one-person LLCs).
   - Bring: DnR's exact legal name and state, the EIN, the articles of organization, Duck's ID. For "what does your business do": "Web development and online media, including a comedy podcast."
   - Some banks close accounts left untouched for a long time; a small transfer or purchase every few months keeps it active.
3. **Assumed name (DBA) "Defacing the Music" for DnR,** so sponsors can pay and sign with "Duck and Rabbit LLC, doing business as Defacing the Music" and checks made out to the show can be deposited. For an Illinois LLC it's filed with the Illinois Secretary of State (short form, a fee, renewed every few years); confirm the current form and fee there.
4. **Point the payouts at DnR's account:** YouTube AdSense, Spotify, sponsors, affiliate programs.

## The one rule
Keep show money out of personal accounts. Mixing them weakens the LLC's protection. Contracts, payments and the bank account all go in DnR's name.
