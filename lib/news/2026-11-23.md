## 1. StubHub sold a concert that doesn't exist
**Happened:** 2026-11-15 (NZ Herald story; the promoters found the listing the week before)
**Wild because:** a ticket site listed a whole concert nobody booked. **Why anyone cares:** everyone has bought tickets online and wondered if they're real.
**On-screen hook text:** They sold tickets to a concert that doesn't exist
**Title:** StubHub listed a concert that was never booked
**Sources:** [NZ Herald: Fans warned after ticket resale platform StubHub advertises non-existent Manukau Ten Tenors concert](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/entertainment/fans-warned-after-ticket-resale-platform-stubhub-advertises-non-existent-manukau-ten-tenors-concert/5VYZGFYLYBCGTOJ4CHOOZL4Y4A/)

StubHub sold tickets to a concert that does not exist. Not sold out. Not cancelled. Never existed.

New Zealand, this month. StubHub had The Ten Tenors, which is exactly what it sounds like, playing March 1st at a venue in south Auckland. The promoters found out from somebody else. The venue told the New Zealand Herald: "We have not listed this event for sale."

The promoters had to put out a statement just to tell people where their own show is: "The actual Auckland concert is at the Aotea Centre on February 28, 2027." Different venue. Different day. The one on StubHub was imaginary.

StubHub took the listing down. The Herald says you could still find it on Google. And the government's Commerce Commission says, "We will complete an assessment of these concerns." Which is the most New Zealand sentence ever written.

[THAT GUY STING] That Guy: whoever looked at a concert with no venue, no promoter and no band, and said, yeah, list it.

## 2. Slayer fans ripped out the chairs
**Happened:** 2026-11-14 (Kia Forum, Inglewood, second sold-out night)
**Wild because:** fans tore out seats to make more room to mosh, and the fire marshal stopped the show. **Why anyone cares:** a metal band telling its fans to calm down is funny to anyone.
**On-screen hook text:** Slayer fans ripped out the seats to mosh. The fire marshal had notes.
**Title:** Slayer stopped a show to tell their fans to behave
**Sources:** [Louder: Slayer's Tom Araya threatens to leave if fans trash venue](https://www.loudersound.com/bands-artists/slayer-tom-araya-threatens-to-leave-if-fans-trash-venue), [The Metalverse: concert review](https://www.themetalverse.net/fucking-slayer-concert-review-and-photos/), [Loudwire](https://loudwire.com/slayer-stop-concert-unruly-crowd-safety-concerns), [TMZ](https://www.tmz.com/2026/11/15/slayer-shuts-down-show-after-mosh-pit-erupts-in-seats/)

Slayer fans ripped the seats out of an arena so they could mosh in them.

November 14th, the Forum in LA. Sold out. The pit is supposed to stay up front. But the people with seats on the floor wanted in on it, so, one reviewer says, they "ripped out some interlocking seats, presumably so they could mosh in the seated section."

Seven songs in, the fire marshal stops the show. Tom Araya, the man who wrote "Angel of Death," turns on the house lights and becomes a substitute teacher: "If you guys want to have fun, you have fun right here in front of us, in the middle of the area where there's NOTHING you can destroy except each other."

And then, the most dad sentence in metal history: "We have to pay for that shit."

He meant it, too: "I'm not joking, man. I'm not joking. Have you ever heard me talk to you like this?" The crowd yelled "No!" After about five minutes, they finished the show. Nobody got hurt.

My favorite detail: Slayer's stage had fire cannons shooting sixty feet in the air. The fire marshal was worried about the chairs.

[THAT GUY STING] That Guy: the first guy in row whatever who looked at his seat and thought, this is in my way.

## 3. Ye allegedly locked a composer in a high school band room
**Happened:** 2026-11-11 (lawsuit filed; the events are from November 2019)
**Wild because:** a lawsuit says Ye locked a composer in a Wyoming high school band room and gave him four days to write an opera. **Why anyone cares:** "locked in a band room until the opera is done" is insane on its own.
**On-screen hook text:** He says Kanye locked him in a high school band room to write an opera
**Title:** Lawsuit: Kanye locked a composer in a band room to write an opera
**Careful:** these are allegations in a lawsuit; nothing's proven, Ye's side hasn't commented. Say "he says" / "the lawsuit says." Story only: nothing about Ye's views or politics, and leave out the complaint's claim about why dancers were fired (racial).
**Sources:** [Cowboy State Daily: Composer says Kanye West locked him in Cody High School, made him write an opera](https://cowboystatedaily.com/2026/11/16/composer-says-kanye-west-locked-him-in-cody-high-school-made-him-write-an-opera/), [Rolling Stone](https://au.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/kanye-west-sued-opera-singer-wyoming-high-school-103335), [Paste](https://www.pastemagazine.com/music/kanye-west-accused-of-locking-musicians-inside-a-high-school-to-write-an-opera), [Wyoming Tribune Eagle / Cody Enterprise](http://www.wyomingnews.com/news/local_news/complaint-ye-locked-musician-in-cody-high-band-room-to-finish-opera/article_53523ac2-06e6-4b5c-8777-47a7031738c5.html)

A composer says Kanye West locked him in a high school band room and told him to write an opera. In four days.

This is a lawsuit filed November 11th, so: allegedly. Ye's side hasn't said anything. But here's what it says.

November 2019. Opera singer Chase Taylor gets hired to sing in Ye's opera "Nebuchadnezzar." He flies to Cody, Wyoming, where Ye had just bought a ranch. They take him to the Cody High School band room. And the opera he was hired to sing in? It doesn't exist yet. He has four days to write it.

The lawsuit: "Defendants locked the door and ordered Taylor to work until the opera was done." Over 150 performers crammed in a band room, musicians sleeping on the floor. Meanwhile, he says, Ye was getting massages. In the band room.

He was also told it was a non-profit religious show. The lawsuit: "That was a lie."

To be fair, the school's band director told the local paper he was in that room almost the whole time and never saw locked doors.

Four days later, the opera premiered at the Hollywood Bowl. It started over two hours late and ran about fifty minutes.

[THAT GUY STING] That Guy: allegedly, the guy getting a massage in a band room while 150 people write his opera.
