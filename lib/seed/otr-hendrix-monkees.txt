## HENDRIX OPENS FOR THE MONKEES
[CLIP: Hendrix vs. "We want Davy!"]
Jimi Hendrix's first big American tour was opening for the Monkees.

The legend: Hendrix got kicked off that tour because a group of patriotic ladies said he was too dirty.

[TALK: Legend or fact? Ask Pat who he thinks booked this.]

Summer 1967. A month earlier, Hendrix lit his guitar on fire at the Monterey Pop Festival and blew everybody's mind. Then he gets booked as the opening act for the Monkees, the TV band, the most kid-friendly band in America.

Whose idea? Micky Dolenz, the Monkees' drummer, saw Hendrix and loved him. And Hendrix's manager saw the publicity. Nobody seems to have asked the twelve year olds.

July 8th, 1967, Jacksonville, Florida. Hendrix walks out at full volume, and an arena full of young girls drowns him out chanting for Davy Jones. Every night. "We want Davy!"

Micky Dolenz, years later: "It must have been incredibly frustrating for Jimi." Mike Nesmith from the Monkees used to sneak out front every night just to watch Hendrix play.

He makes it seven shows. The last one is in Forest Hills, New York, and Nesmith remembered Hendrix flipping off the crowd, cursing, and walking off. And then he's gone.

Hendrix to the NME on the way out: "I think they're replacing me with Mickey Mouse!"

Now the cover story. A music writer traveling with the tour, Lillian Roxon, wrote a joke press release. It said the Daughters of the American Revolution, a very proper women's group, pressured the promoters to drop Hendrix because his act was "too erotic."

And newspapers printed it. As fact.

Verdict: LEGEND. The ladies never did anything. A writer made it up as a joke, and it was a better story than "the crowd only wanted Davy."

Hendrix played Woodstock two years later. Davy Jones did not.
