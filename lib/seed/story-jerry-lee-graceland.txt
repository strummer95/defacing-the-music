## SUN
In 1956, Jerry Lee goes to Memphis, to Sun Records, Sam Phillips' little studio where Elvis got started. He gets work there as a session piano player.
On December 4th, 1956, he's playing on a Carl Perkins session when Elvis Presley walks in. Elvis had left Sun for RCA, but he dropped by to say hi. They start jamming gospel songs. Johnny Cash shows up. And somebody leaves the tape running.
The next day the Memphis Press-Scimitar runs a story and calls them the Million Dollar Quartet. Elvis, Johnny Cash, Carl Perkins, and the new kid on piano.
So that's the first time Jerry Lee and Elvis are in a room together. Singing hymns around a piano at the studio where both of them got their start.
Twenty years later, one of them's going to be upstairs, and the other one's going to be at the gate.

## THE GATE
November 23rd. A little before three a.m.
He comes back to Graceland. Different car. Since the Rolls is upside down, it's a brand new white Lincoln Continental.
The guard on duty is family. Most accounts say it's Elvis's cousin, Harold Loyd. And the guard has already been told: don't let this guy in. Nobody ever pinned down who gave that order. Might just be what you do when the same drunk guy shows up twice in one day.
So the guard calls up to the house. In Loyd's retelling, Elvis gets on the line and wants to know what the hell is going on down there. And Loyd tells him Jerry Lee is "sittin' in his car down here outside the gate, wavin' a derringer pistol and raisin' hell."

## WHAT JERRY LEE SAID LATER
Jerry Lee always said the gun slid off the dash, he never waved it at anybody, and he never, ever meant to shoot Elvis. In his seventies, he sat down with the writer Rick Bragg for two summers of interviews for his book, "Jerry Lee Lewis: His Own Story." And when he gets to Graceland, you can still hear it bugging him.
He told Bragg he thought Elvis told the cops to lock him up. He said the idea that Elvis would be scared of him, as well as they knew each other, was ridiculous. And then he said this about Elvis: "He was a coward. He hurt me. That did."
He never saw Elvis again. Nine months later, on August 16th, 1977, Elvis died. At Graceland. He was forty two.
So the last time those two were anywhere near each other, one was upstairs, and the other one was in a police car with a cut on his face. Twenty years after they sang hymns around a piano at Sun.
