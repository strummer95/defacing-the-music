## THE LEGEND
London has thousands of wild bright-green parakeets. The story goes that Jimi Hendrix released a pair on Carnaby Street in 1968, and every one of them descends from Jimi's birds.

## VERDICT (to confirm)
LEGEND. Scientists actually studied where the parakeets came from (pet escapes and releases over decades, sightings going back long before 1968) and found nothing behind the Hendrix story. Other versions blame the film The African Queen.

## THE ANGLE
Anyone gets it, no music knowledge needed. The fun is that it's such a perfect rock legend that people wanted it to be true, and the rival origin stories are just as dumb.

## RESEARCH TO DO
- The study that tested the Hendrix theory (who did it, when, what they found).
- How many wild parakeets London has now, and the other origin legends.
- Where the Hendrix version first showed up in print.
