## IDEA (main episode, not a script yet)
Duck asked for this one (Nov 25): "I def need a ICP episode, maybe just about how the Gathering came to be, but it's an episode to be careful with." Research before writing; everything below is from memory and must be confirmed.

## THE ANGLE
How the Gathering of the Juggalos came to be: two guys from Detroit with face paint build their own festival because nobody else would book them, and it turns into one of the strangest annual events in America. The chain of decisions (How Did This Get Made) is the fun: who said yes, where they held it, what they added every year. The ending should feel inevitable: of course it ended up on a campground in southern Illinois with a wrestling ring.

## CAREFUL (Duck: "an episode to be careful with")
- The fans are never the joke. Juggalos are a real community and the show punches at decisions, not at people for being poor, rural or into this music.
- The FBI listed Juggalos as a gang in 2011, ICP sued, and there was a march in Washington in 2017: legal and political. One straight sentence at most, no take (CLAUDE.md hard rule), or leave it out.
- People have been hurt and some have died at or around Gatherings: if it comes up, a short straight acknowledgment, never the joke.
- Drugs were part of the scene: mention straight, don't build bits on them.
- No profanity-heavy lyric quotes on air; paraphrase.
