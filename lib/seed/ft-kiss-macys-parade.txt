## THE STORY
Macy's Thanksgiving Parade, 2014. Paul Stanley says KISS was shown sketches of a huge float.

It was supposed to be the Gibson guitar float. Then, their manager Doc McGhee says, somebody noticed the night before: Paul doesn't play Gibson. So, no Gibson float.

What they got, in McGhee's words, "is literally like, 25 feet long with two microphones on it."

Paul, years later, on Twitter: "Instead we got an SUV with a U-Haul attached and two unimpressed escorts."

McGhee's reaction: "ohhhh, this is how you get fired."

He didn't get fired. He still manages them.

So if your turkey comes out a little small today, relax. KISS got a U-Haul.
