## IGGY POP'S RIDER
[CLIP: Iggy wants seven dwarves]
Iggy Pop's tour contract asks for seven dwarves dressed like the ones from the Disney movie.

The legend: Iggy Pop has the craziest rider in rock.

[TALK: Legend or fact? Ask Pat what's the one thing he'd put in his.]

A rider is the part of a band's contract that tells the venue what to have ready. Usually it's boring. Towels. Water. Power.

In 2012, the Smoking Gun posted Iggy and the Stooges' rider. And it is not boring.

Seven dwarves, "dressed up as those dwarves out of that marvelous Walt Disney film." With a note: "It's attitude, more than altitude that's important here."

A Bob Hope impersonator. That one's apparently been on there for years. And if they can't find one, they'll settle for a belly dancer, and the writer adds, "she can use my belly to dance on."

Food. They want ham "sliced directly off a pig's bottom." And then a "bottle of extremely hot pepper sauce so that no one has to actually taste the sliced ham."

So you buy the ham, and then you buy the thing that makes sure nobody tastes the ham.

And there's a section about Ron Howard. The director. The guy from "Happy Days." The rider says he's "Got one of those faces you'd never get tired of punching." Ron Howard is not on the tour. Ron Howard has nothing to do with any of this.

[Pause.]

Now the twist. Iggy didn't write it. His stage manager, Jos Grain, did. And Grain claims he didn't really write it either. He says he "merely took dictation---from a ouija board!"

Verdict: FACT. It's real, it's in writing, and promoters had to read every word to find out where to put the towels.

Which might be the whole point. If you can get through the dwarves and the pig's bottom, you'll find the part about the power.
