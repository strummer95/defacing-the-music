## IDEA (Off The Record, not a script yet)
Duck approved this one for future work (Nov 23). Research it before writing; the verdict below is Claude's best guess from memory. Length: about 15 minutes on its own, or about 8 to 10 minutes as one of three in a 30-minute episode.

## THE LEGEND
KISS put out a Marvel comic in 1977, and the legend says the red ink was mixed with the band's own blood.

## VERDICT (to confirm)
FACT. The band had blood drawn, it was mixed into the red ink at the printing plant, and there was a notary there to prove it. It was a marketing stunt and it worked.

## THE ANGLE
It sounds like the fakest story on the list, which is exactly why it goes here. Who pitched this, who said yes, and who was the notary who signed off on "yes, that's their blood"?

## RESEARCH TO DO
- The 1977 Marvel Super Special: who came up with the blood idea, where the printing happened, and the notary details.
- How it was sold to fans at the time (the ads, the price, today's money).
- What the band said later.

## SHORTS HOOK
"KISS put their actual blood in a comic book. With a notary watching."
