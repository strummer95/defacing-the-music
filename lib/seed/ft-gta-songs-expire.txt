## THE STORY
Rockstar doesn't own the music in Grand Theft Auto. It rents it. The licenses run out. And when they do, the songs come out of the game. Even the copy you already paid for.

2012. Vice City gets pulled off Steam over a Michael Jackson song. When it comes back, nearly ten songs are gone. Go play the remaster today. No "Billie Jean."

2014. An update rips seventeen songs out of San Andreas. From copies people had already bought.

2018. GTA IV turns ten. Ten-year licenses, ten years later, almost to the day. Dozens of songs, gone. David Bowie. AC/DC. Deleted.

So that song you love driving to in GTA 6 right now? It's on a clock. Ten years from now, your favorite car might go quiet.
