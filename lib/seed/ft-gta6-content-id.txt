So now the robot thinks anybody playing GTA 6 is stealing from Frank. Big channels get flagged. Even streams with the music turned off. One streamer said, "Ppl are losing 10 hour streams to him."

DarkViperAU, one of the biggest GTA channels, messaged him: "Yo my dude, you have put your GTA 6 playthrough in the Content ID system, and so are claiming GTA 6 gameplay livestreams. Please fix."

Frank's apology: "I'm really sorry. I'm not filing these claims manually, and I don't own any rights to GTA 6."

YouTube says it fixed it. But for one weekend, the biggest video game ever made apparently belonged to one reggaeton guy.
