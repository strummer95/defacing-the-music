## THE LEGEND
Everybody knows the joke: Van Halen were such spoiled rock stars their contract banned brown M&M's from the backstage bowl. People still use it as shorthand for a diva demand.

## VERDICT (to confirm)
FACT, and it flips the whole story. The clause was real, but it was a test, buried deep in a huge technical rider. If the band's crew walked in and found brown M&M's, it meant the venue hadn't read the contract closely, so the stage weight limits, power and rigging might be wrong too, and they'd check everything. It went from "spoiled" to "genius safety trick."

## THE ANGLE
The twist is the whole bit: start with the diva version everyone knows, let Pat call them brats, then reveal it was a smoke detector made of candy.
