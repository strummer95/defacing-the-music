## OZZY AND THE ANTS
[CLIP: Ozzy's ants]
Ozzy snorted a line of ants. You already know the answer. Or do you?

The legend: on tour with Mötley Crüe, Ozzy Osbourne snorted a line of live ants off the ground through a straw.

[TALK: Legend or fact? If Pat heard the bat episode, he's already guessing.]

1984. Ozzy's on tour, Mötley Crüe is opening, and everybody's at a hotel pool. Ozzy's wearing a dress. Nikki Sixx tells it in the Mötley Crüe book, The Dirt:

"I handed [Ozzy] the straw, and he walked over to a crack in the sidewalk and bent over it. I saw a long column of ants, marching to a little dugout built where the pavement met the dirt. And as I thought, 'No, he wouldn't,' he did."

He sent the whole line "up his nose with a single, monstrous snort." What happened next at that pool involved a puddle, and it's not for this show. It was a gross-out contest, and Nikki says Ozzy won.

Now, is it true? Ozzy's own book, in 2009: "People always ask me if I snorted a line of ants. I can't remember whether I did or not, but it's quite possible." Fourteen years later, on his family's podcast: "Yes. Yes, I did. I was there. I did it." Sharon, same episode: "I was not there, thank God."

But here's the twist. Jake E. Lee, Ozzy's guitar player, was at that pool. And he says: "I was there, and I never saw ants... He snorted a little spider." And then he says the greatest sentence in rock history: "It's a minor detail."

Even the guys who were there can't agree where it happened. Tommy Lee said a Ritz Carlton in Florida. Ten years later, he said the Four Seasons in Dallas.

Verdict: FACT. Probably. Ants or a spider. Florida or Texas. Either way, Ozzy put a bug up his nose.

And who handed him the straw? Nikki Sixx.
