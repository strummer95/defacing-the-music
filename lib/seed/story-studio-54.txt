## IDEA (main episode, not a script yet)
Duck asked for this one (Nov 25): "maybe something about Studio 54, if it can be related enough to music." It can: the music hook is the spine. Research before writing; everything below is from memory and must be confirmed.

## THE MUSIC HOOK (the cold open and the clip)
Nile Rodgers and Bernard Edwards of Chic got invited to Studio 54 by Grace Jones on New Year's Eve 1977, then got turned away at the door. They went home, started jamming on what they wanted to shout at the doorman, and it became "Le Freak," one of the biggest-selling singles of the era. Find Nile Rodgers's own telling (his memoir, Le Freak, and interviews) for exact quotes.

## THE CHAIN OF DECISIONS (How Did This Get Made)
- Steve Rubell and Ian Schrager open it in 1977 in an old opera house / TV studio, and the whole business is the velvet rope: who gets in.
- The famous nights: Bianca Jagger's birthday on a white horse; the celebrities; the door stories (who got rejected and how they tried to get in).
- Rubell tells a reporter only the Mafia makes more money. Soon after, the IRS raids the club (December 1978) and finds cash hidden in the ceiling and walls, plus a second set of books.
- Rubell and Schrager go to prison for tax evasion. The farewell party before they report (February 1980) has its own famous guest list and a Diana Ross moment to confirm.
- Same era as Disco Demolition (July 1979): Studio 54 is the top of disco, Comiskey is the backlash. A good callback if both get made.

## THAT GUY CANDIDATES
Steve Rubell (bragging to the press about the money), or the doorman who turned away the guys who then wrote the club's biggest anthem.

## CAREFUL
- Drug use was everywhere: mention it straight, don't make it the joke.
- Rubell died in 1989 of AIDS-related illness; if it comes up, one straight, respectful line in Where Are They Now, nothing more.
- Keep it on the music, the door and the money; skip celebrity gossip that isn't sourced.
