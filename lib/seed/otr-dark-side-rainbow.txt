## THE LEGEND
Start The Dark Side of the Moon on the MGM lion's third roar and it lines up with The Wizard of Oz: songs change when scenes change, lyrics match what's on screen. The legend says Pink Floyd did it on purpose.

## VERDICT (to confirm)
LEGEND, according to the band, who've said for decades it's a coincidence. But it's a huge story anyway (Duck: "worth talking about cuz its such a big story"): it spread through dorm rooms and early internet forums, got big enough that a TV channel reportedly aired the two together, and people still argue about it.

## THE ANGLE
Not "is it real" so much as "how did this take over the world." The rabbit hole is the people timing it to the lion's roar and the list of "matches" fans swear by.
