## THE FOO FIGHTERS' COLORING BOOK
[CLIP: The Foo Fighters' rider is a coloring book]
The Foo Fighters' tour contract came with a coloring book.

The legend: rock stars only send ridiculous riders because they're divas.

[TALK: Legend or fact? Ask Pat what a band has to do to earn a coloring book.]

2011 world tour. The Smoking Gun posts the Foo Fighters' rider, and it's fifty two pages long. And a lot of those pages are coloring pages. And word searches.

The catering section has drawings. On one page there's a "LAME ENTREE," a sandwich with a side of rocks. Next to it, an "AWESOME ENTREE," a steak and a baked potato. Pick one, venue. Choose wisely.

It asks for Cornish game hens, because, quote, "They make a boy feel fancy." The Smoking Gun says it bans light sabers, garden gnomes and tridents from the venue. Somebody had a reason for the tridents.

There's a whole section on socks, t-shirts and movies, labeled, word for word, "I'M NOT GETTING THESE DOUCHEBAGS DVD'S + UNDERWEAR!"

So who wrote it? Not Dave Grohl. Their tour manager, Gus Brandt. Grohl told Rolling Stone, "That's his personality and sense of humor, he's a wicked smartass."

And here's the part that flips it. Grohl says the whole thing is really about the crew getting fed. His words: "those guys show up at fucking seven o'clock in the morning."

Verdict: LEGEND. It's not a diva move. It's a tour manager making sure a crew of tired guys gets a steak instead of a sandwich with rocks. He just figured nobody reads a fifty two page contract unless there's something to color.

Same trick as Van Halen's brown M&M's, basically. Make it weird enough that you know they read it.
