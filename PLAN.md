# Defacing the Music: 3-month growth plan

Goal: build a YouTube channel that grows every week, with Spotify and Apple along for the ride.
Push people to YouTube (@DefacingTheMusic) first; defacingthemusic.com is the one link that covers everything.

## The weekly rhythm (every week, all 12 weeks)
| Day | Do |
|---|---|
| Mon | Record the episode (teleprompter in the admin). Upload the raw file in admin > Uploads. |
| Tue | Edit: cut ums (Descript "Remove filler words"), Claude fixes volume + adds the name bar. |
| Wed | Thumbnail + title + description (Claude drafts). Schedule the YouTube premiere. |
| Thu | Publish: YouTube (main), Spotify (video), Apple/Amazon follow automatically. Instagram post + Story link sticker. |
| Fri, Sun, Tue | One Short/Reel/TikTok each: 30-60 sec clips cut from that week's episode, hook in the first second, captions on. |
| Daily, 10 min | Reply to every comment. Early on, every reply counts. |

Every episode outro: "Subscribe on YouTube, follow on Spotify, and get on the Guest List at defacingthemusic.com."

## Month 1: get the machine running (weeks 1-4)
- Episodes: 1 Keith Moon, 2 Woodstock '99, 3 Ozzy and the bat (all scripted), 4 Disco Demolition (Chicago).
- Lock the workflow above so a full week takes a few hours, not a few days.
- Set up the YouTube channel: banner (Claude can make it), "About" text, a trailer (Episode 0), and a playlist with every episode.
- Post the theme video and Episode 0 clips on TikTok/Reels/Shorts to seed the new accounts.
- Turn on the Guest List: share defacingthemusic.com in every bio.

## Month 2: find what clicks (weeks 5-8)
- Episodes: Dave Matthews bus (Chicago), Fyre Festival, Milli Vanilli, Sex Pistols on the Today show.
- Check YouTube Studio each week: which thumbnails get clicked (CTR) and where people stop watching. Do more of what works.
- Ask for stories in every episode and pin the best comment.
- Reach out to Chicago music pages, local venues and classic rock/metal groups with clips that fit them (the Chicago episodes are the easy pitch).
- Remake the theme song on a paid Suno month so you own it (needed before any ads or sponsors).

## Month 3: build toward money (weeks 9-12)
- Episodes: Jerry Lee Lewis at Graceland, Montreux fire / "Smoke on the Water", Metallica and GN'R Montreal, GG Allin.
- Merch test: warning-sign logo shirt and sticker through a print-on-demand shop (no upfront cost). Link it from the site.
- Start a simple email to the Guest List when each episode drops.
- Review the numbers against YouTube's Partner Program bar (1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours in 12 months, or 10M Shorts views in 90 days; check YouTube Studio > Earn for the current rules) and plan the next quarter.

## Realistic expectations
Most new shows grow slowly for the first few months, then pick up once there's a back catalog and one or two clips catch on.
Consistency beats everything: 12 episodes in 12 weeks puts you ahead of most podcasts that quit by episode 3.
