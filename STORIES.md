# Defacing the Music: story list

Every idea for the show in one place. Fact-check each one before scripting (the first AI chat's pitches had invented details).
Titles follow the format: "Due To ..." / "Who ...?"

## Scripted (in the admin teleprompter)
1. Keith Moon, The Smothers Brothers Comedy Hour, 1967. Due To Excessive Flash Powder / Who Gave The Who Explosives?
2. Woodstock '99, Limp Bizkit and "Break Stuff". Due To The Venue Being Ripped Apart / Who Asked Fred Durst to Be a Therapist?
3. Ozzy and the bat, Des Moines, 1982 (plus the dove, 1981). Due To a Possible Rabies Infection / Who Let That Guy Bring a Bat?

## Up next (fun all the way through)
- Disco Demolition Night, Comiskey Park, Chicago, 1979. Records blown up in center field, fans storm the field, White Sox forfeit. Hometown episode. Due To Excessive Disco / Who Let a DJ Blow Up Center Field?
- Dave Matthews Band tour bus, Chicago, 2004. Driver empties the toilet tank through the Kinzie Street bridge grate onto a tour boat. Settlement, guilty plea. Due To a Septic Tank Over the River / Who Let the Bus Driver Find the Bridge?
- Fyre Festival, 2017. Luxury island festival becomes disaster tents and a cheese sandwich; Billy McFarland convicted of fraud. Due To a Cheese Sandwich / Who Sold Them Paradise?
- Milli Vanilli, 1989-1990. Backing track skips live; they didn't sing on the records; Grammy revoked. (Rob Pilatus died in 1998: one straight line.) Due To a Skipping Track / Who Let the Tape Run?
- Sex Pistols on the Today show with Bill Grundy, 1976. Grundy goads them into swearing on early-evening TV; tabloids report a lorry driver kicking in his own TV. Due To Language / Who Dared the Sex Pistols?
- Jerry Lee Lewis at the gates of Graceland, 1976. Drunk, 3 a.m., loaded gun, demanding to see Elvis. Elvis was home. He did not come down. Due To an Unannounced Visitor / Who Let Jerry Lee Drive to Graceland?
- Montreux Casino fire, 1971. Flare gun at a Frank Zappa show, casino burns, Deep Purple writes "Smoke on the Water". Due To a Flare Gun Indoors / Who Brought a Flare Gun to a Zappa Show?
- Metallica and Guns N' Roses, Montreal, 1992. Hetfield burned by pyro, Axl ends the GN'R set early, riot. Due To Pyro and a Sore Throat / Who Stood James Hetfield on the Flamethrower?
- GG Allin. Nearly every show ended in a riot, police or a hospital. Due To Everything / Who Kept Booking GG Allin?

## Darker (careful tone: people died or were hurt)
- Mayhem and Norwegian black metal, early 1990s. Suicide, church burnings, a murder.
- Bjork's stalker, 1996. A mailed acid bomb, intercepted by police.
