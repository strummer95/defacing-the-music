# Off The Record: otr-fake-fleetwood-mac

Written Nov 28 2026 for Duck's themed B-Sides (Tour Disasters / Riders).

Bob Weston affair with Mick Fleetwood's wife, Fleetwood canceled the US tour; Clifford Davis assembled a new lineup (Elmer Gantry, Kirby Gregory, Paul Martinez, Craig Collinge drumming the first show); Gantry says Davis told them Fleetwood would join; first show Jan 16 1974 Pittsburgh; promoter Rich Engler: Davis said "They're here."; Engler: crowd "was going wild and loving it"; Jan 26 New York show supported by Kiss and Silverhead; Bob Welch "a rip-off" (Rolling Stone); Davis to Rolling Stone "This band is my band. This band has always been my band."; about two years of legal fallout; Fleetwood memoir "To this day, I don't know the names of the musicians involved and I don't wanna know."; Welch persuaded the band to form its own company and move operations to the US, setting up the Buckingham/Nicks era: Ultimate Classic Rock.
