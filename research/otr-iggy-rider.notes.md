# Off The Record: otr-iggy-rider

Written Nov 28 2026 for Duck's themed B-Sides (Tour Disasters / Riders).

Iggy and the Stooges 2012 rider, written by stage manager Jos Grain, posted by The Smoking Gun: seven dwarves "dressed up as those dwarves out of that marvelous Walt Disney film", "It's attitude, more than altitude that's important here.", longstanding Bob Hope impersonator request with belly dancer fallback "she can use my belly to dance on.", ham "sliced directly off a pig's bottom" and "bottle of extremely hot pepper sauce so that no one has to actually taste the sliced ham.", Ron Howard "Got one of those faces you'd never get tired of punching.", "merely took dictation---from a ouija board!".
