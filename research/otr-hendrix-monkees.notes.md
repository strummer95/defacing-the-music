# Off The Record: otr-hendrix-monkees

Written Nov 28 2026 for Duck's themed B-Sides (Tour Disasters / Riders).

Dolenz suggested Hendrix after Monterey; tour began July 8 1967 Jacksonville; seven shows, last at Forest Hills; crowds chanted for Davy Jones; Nesmith snuck to the front to watch and recalled Hendrix flipping off the crowd and walking off; Lillian Roxon's tongue-in-cheek press release blaming the Daughters of the American Revolution ("too erotic") printed as fact: Ultimate Classic Rock. Hendrix to NME "I think they're replacing me with Mickey Mouse!" and Dolenz (Telegraph) "It must have been incredibly frustrating for Jimi.": Louder. "We want Davy!" chant wording is the widely reported chant (UCR says fans chanted for Davy Jones). Hendrix set his guitar on fire at Monterey (June 1967) and played Woodstock (1969): general history.
