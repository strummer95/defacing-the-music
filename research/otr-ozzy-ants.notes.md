# Off The Record: Ozzy and the Ants (research notes, Nov 23, 2026)

Sources list: lib/sources/otr-ozzy-ants.json. Story block only; waits for two more stories.
- Told by Nikki Sixx in The Dirt (2001); 1984 tour (Bark at the Moon), Motley Crue opening; hotel pool (Florida per UCR).
- Book passage (exact as quoted by Blabbermouth; copies differ slightly, "little dugout" vs "little sand dugout": check a physical copy before recording): "I handed [Ozzy] the straw, and he walked over to a crack in the sidewalk and bent over it. I saw a long column of ants, marching to a little dugout built where the pavement met the dirt. And as I thought, 'No, he wouldn't,' he did." ... "sent the entire line of ants tickling up his nose with a single, monstrous snort." Script quotes "up his nose with a single, monstrous snort."
- The rest of the scene (urine) left out on purpose.
- Ozzy, I Am Ozzy (2009, exact via Rolling Stone India): "People always ask me if I snorted a line of ants. I can't remember whether I did or not, but it's quite possible."
- The Osbournes Podcast, Oct 2023 (exact via Blabbermouth): "Yes. Yes, I did. I was there. I did it." Sharon: "I was not there, thank God."
- Jake E. Lee, Tone-Talk, April 2019 (exact): "I was there, and I never saw ants... He snorted a little spider..." "It's a minor detail."
- Tommy Lee: Ritz Carlton in Florida (Q, 2014); Four Seasons in Dallas (Theo Von, Aug 2024).
- That Guy: Nikki Sixx handed him the straw (his own telling).
