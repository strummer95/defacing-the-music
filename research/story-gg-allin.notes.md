# story-gg-allin: fact-check notes

Research note: direct page fetches were blocked in this session, so facts were checked via web-search result summaries of the pages below. Spot check before recording. Tone choices: no graphic detail beyond one "bodily functions" reference and "blood and other stuff" in the cab scene; assault victim and the death handled straight in THE SERIOUS PART.

- Born Jesus Christ Allin, Aug 29, 1956, Lancaster, NH; renamed Kevin Michael Allin by his mother (birth certificate date March 2, 1962; one source says 1966, script uses 1962). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GG_Allin ; https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0021359/bio/

## "The story goes" pile
- Father claiming Jesus visited him and foretold the baby's greatness: in the biographies, but it's family lore.
- That he kept being in jail whenever the suicide date came round: only 1989 is documented; the pattern is framed as "the story goes."
- Whether any cab stopped: explicitly flagged as unknowable; the "good cab driver" in THAT GUY is framed as imaginary.

## Checked and left out
- Graphic onstage details (feces, self-harm specifics, funeral body details): deliberately left out per brief.
- Funeral specifics (clothing, party at the grave, photo on album): sourced but gross-out; left out.
- Drummer Dino Sex playing naked, labels (Homestead/ROIR), NYU appearances: unverified, left out.
- Name of the apartment host (John Handley, fan-wiki only) and exact address: inconsistent, left out.
- Phillips' budget (~$12,000): single-source, left out.
- No explicit drug-use glamor; death given one plain line plus context.
