# Off The Record: Oz, Phil and the Parakeets (research notes, Nov 23, 2026)

Sources list: lib/sources/otr-vol-2.json.

## Dark Side of the Rainbow
- Usenet alt.music.pink-floyd; Charles (Charlie) Savage, 19, intern, Fort Wayne Journal Gazette, Aug 1, 1995 (earliest documentation; he says he "didn't invent this idea"). Now NYT national security and legal reporter.
- Savage (exact, via UCR/Mojo): "That quirky little article I wrote when I was 19 is going to follow me around for the rest of my life."
- Boston DJ George Taylor Morris, WZLX (1996 or 1997). Capitol (exact, Spokesman-Review 1997): "Capitol did not have anything to do with this."
- TCM aired it July 3, 2000 with the album on the SAP channel.
- Matches (Dave Kopel, Relix 1997): fence / "balanced on the biggest wave"; "Money" at the switch to color; heartbeat at the Tin Man's chest.
- Waters on Joe Rogan (UCR, Oct 2022, exact): "Bullshit," "Nothing to do with anyone in Pink Floyd or anyone who wrote or recorded any of the music," "Maybe it's cosmic coincidence!" Script shortens the second quote at a natural break.
- Parsons (iHeart 2020, exact as reported): "a fabrication by someone who had much too much time on their hands"; "We didn't have VCRs back then; how could we have done it?"
- Gilmour, Fallon, Nov 7, 2024 (exact): "Well, of course it was." "There was no planning that out. I only heard about it years later."
- Not used: Nick Mason "Sound of Music" line (unverified as exact).

## In the Air Tonight
- Legend versions (boat, beach, front-row ticket, spotlight, arrest): Today I Found Out, Mental Floss, Songfacts.
- Eminem "Stan" references it; exact lyric disputed, so the script only says he put it in the song.

- Hangover (Mike Tyson), Cadbury gorilla ad 2007 (Mental Floss).
- Divorce details disputed by his ex-wife; the script just says he was going through a divorce, in his own words.

## Hendrix's parakeets
- Heald et al., "Understanding the origins of the ring-necked parakeet in the UK," Journal of Zoology, Dec 2019; lead Steven Le Comber, Queen Mary University of London. Geographic profiling on 5,000+ sightings 1968 to 2018. No hotspot at Carnaby Street, Worton Hall Studios (The African Queen) or Syon Park. 3,697 newspaper pages mentioning "parakeet" 1804 to 2008, no release tied to the myths. Oldest Hendrix/African Queen reference found: BBC News online, Sept 2005.
- Sightings: Norfolk 1855; a pair "with five offspring" Lincoln's Inn Fields 1886.
- Parrot disease panics 1929-31 and 1952; Lancashire Evening Post, Dec 30, 1929 (exact, as quoted in the paper): Berlin authorities "recommended people take precautions and not to allow parrots to kiss or bite them".
- Le Comber (exact, QMUL): "...the spread of parakeets in the UK is likely a consequence of repeated releases and introductions, and nothing to do with publicity stunts by musicians or movie stars." Script quotes from "a consequence".
- Population: "around 30,000 in and around London" is a soft estimate (Time Out via BBC Wild London). Script says "estimates."
