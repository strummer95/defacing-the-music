# Fresh Tracks: StubHub listed a concert that was never booked

Happened 2026-11-15. Researched Nov 23, 2026; picks, quotes and caveats are in `lib/news/2026-11-23.md` and summarized from the research reports there. Sources: `lib/sources/ft-stubhub-phantom-concert.json`.

## Who did what (Nov 23, Duck found the first telling confusing)
- Real show: booked by co-promoters Showcase Entertainment and Pacific Entertainment, Aotea Centre, Feb 28, 2027 (NZ Promoters Association statement, NZ Herald).
- Fake listing: StubHub advertised a March 1 Ten Tenors show at Due Drop Events Centre, Manukau. The promoters were told "last week" (before Nov 15) that StubHub was selling tickets to it. The venue: "We have not listed this event for sale."
- Who created the listing: unknown. StubHub and the band's management were contacted; the Herald reports no response.
- No report says anyone bought tickets, so the script says "was selling," not "sold."
- Taken down; still findable via Google that Saturday. Commerce Commission: two complaints, "We will complete an assessment of these concerns."
- Duck (Nov 23) read it as StubHub doing it on its own. Not established: StubHub is a resale marketplace; the listing came either from StubHub or from a seller using StubHub. The script says exactly that ("either StubHub made up a concert, or StubHub let some random person make up a concert"). What is established: no legitimate party (band, promoters, venue) asked for it.
