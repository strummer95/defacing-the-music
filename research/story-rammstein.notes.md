## CORRECTION to the idea note
- The arrest was NOT on the Family Values Tour. It was Saturday, June 5, 1999, the Worcester Palladium (just under 3,000 capacity), Rammstein's own headlining Sehnsucht tour; openers Skunk Anansie and Soulfly; night before Hammerstein Ballroom NYC, night after Montreal Metropolis. They had played the Worcester Centrum on Family Values about nine months earlier with no arrest.
  - https://www.loudersound.com/features/spunk-willies-and-arrests-remembering-rammsteins-most-notorious-show-ever (Metal Hammer, Joe Daly, Oct 18, 2022)
  - https://ramm.wiki/05.06.1999_(concert) (setlist, 298th show, chronology)
  - https://www.rammsteinworld.com/en/live/concerts/1999-06-05-worcester-ma-the-palladium
  - Contemporary dating: MTV News, "Rammstein's Act Lands Two Members In Jail," June 7, 1999 (cited by Wikipedia; archive URL web.archive.org/web/20041116201641/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1433611/19990607/rammstein.jhtml); Yahoo Music, Stephen Peters, "Rammstein Members Busted For Simulated Sex Act," June 8, 1999; Deseret News June 11, 1999 (below).
  - Conflict: Wikipedia's Christian Lorenz article and one Metal Hammer piece (https://www.loudersound.com/features/amerika-ist-wunderbar-how-rammstein-conquered-the-usa) say Sept 23, 1998 on Family Values. The 1999 dated reports and Flake's account settle it as June 5, 1999.

## The arrest (facts used)
- Charge: lewd and lascivious behavior, over the "Bück dich" act. Police came backstage after the show. (Louder 2022; Deseret News 1999.) Fan site gives German charge as "Erregung öffentlichen Ärgernisses." Till in 2015 calls it "Indecent exposure" (in quotes, in the RS interview).
- Police: Sgt. Thomas Radula, Worcester PD. MTV News (via search summary of the MTV page): Lindemann, 30, simulating sex with Lorenz, 26, onstage using a "fake phallic object that shot water at the crowd." Wikipedia (Christian Lorenz) words it "using a phallic object that shot water over the crowd". Script uses the MTV wording, framed "as MTV News quoted it". Could not open the MTV original to verify word for word.
- Both fully clothed; Flake on a leash on all fours; act on a well-lit riser; liquid started as milk, later Pernod and water; quote "eliminated the malodorous challenges of dousing oneself in dairy each night" is Metal Hammer's writer (Louder 2022).
- Holy Cross: police asked the college for help; a German-language professor (she) volunteered to assist until attorneys arrived. Only source: Louder 2022. Professor not named.
- Band statement, exact, printed by the Deseret News (released to MTV News): "While they strongly deny any criminal wrongdoing, they have retained local counsel and intend to respect the laws of the city in question by seeing this matter through to its logical conclusion." https://www.deseret.com/1999/6/11/19450064/german-band-rammstein-to-play-at-fairpark-tuesday/
- Released Sunday morning on $25 bail (MTV, per search summary and Wikipedia).
- Outcome, conflicting: RammWiki cites Billboard, Aug 7, 1999: "After three rounds of negotiations, the sentence was reduced to $100 and six months' probation." (Billboard issue itself not retrieved.) Louder 2022: "paid a token fine of $25 each." Wikipedia (Bück dich): $200 each. Songfacts: $125. Till in 2015 confirms probation ("why I have probation"). Script says Billboard via RammWiki = $100 + six months' probation, and that other retellings differ.
- RammWiki also says "five hours" in jail (citing the documentary); Flake's own account says overnight. Script follows Flake.

## Flake's own account (German, translated by Claude)
Source: https://www.rammsteinontour.com/concert/199910/ ("Flake über die Verhaftung in Worchester"). The opening paragraph is a third-person tour write-up (injuries), then "die Verhaftung aus Flakes Sicht". The site does not say where it was first published (likely a 1999 fan club newsletter; unconfirmed). Script calls it "a German write-up of it from that summer, with Flake's own account of the arrest in it". All script quotes from it are labeled translated.
- "Doch die Show findet wie jeden Abend statt." = "But the show goes on like every night." (intro writer, not Flake)
- Injuries before June 5: Flake fell out of the boat, cut his head (Platzwunde); Till jumped off stage during "Weißes Fleisch," twisted his knee, "Jetzt humpelt er mit Krücken durch die Gegend."
- "Nach dem Konzert in Worcester sagte mir Nicolai (Tourmanager), daß Till und ich verhaftet werden würden. Ich hielt es zunächst für einen Witz. Dann sah ich aber, wie etwa acht grimmig aussehende Polizisten ins Backstage kamen, und wir mußten richtig betteln, damit wir unter Aufsicht noch schnell duschen durften."
- "Dann bekamen wir Handschellen angelegt und wurden in eine Art Hundefängerwagen gesteckt und ins Gefängnis gefahren." (dogcatcher's van)
- Holding cell empty apart from a drain; gave up glasses and shoes; photos and fingerprints; single cells with a bare wooden bunk.
- "Das war nicht das, was ich mir unter einer After-Show Party vorstellte."
- "Die Mitgefangenen, die zum Teil direkt von einer Schießerei kamen, betrunken waren und unter Drogen standen, veranstalteten einen Höllenlärm, brüllten, schlugen gegen die Wände und stöhnten so, daß an Schlafen nicht zu denken war."
- "Später erfuhren wir, daß es Glück war, daß wir keine Decke bekamen, denn die Leute mit Decken mußten bis zum Montag auf den Haftrichter warten"
- Signed promise to appear voluntarily; left late for Canada.
- "So flogen wir drei Tage später wieder nach Worcester zur Kautions-Verhandlung. Der Staatsanwalt verlangte 10.000 Dollar pro "piece" wie er formulierte." "piece" is in English, in quotes, in the original. Lawyer got bail waived.
- "Nicolai (Tourmanager)": no surname given. The band's production manager from about 1997 was Nicolai Sabottka (pyro). Script does NOT say they're the same person.

## After / band reaction
- Till, Rolling Stone (Kory Grow, June 2015, "Sex, Pills & Metal", reprint https://rollingstoneindia.com/?p=56067): asked about a 1999 Denver "Bück dich": "Yeah, yeah. I got arrested for that in Maine. So we went to jail for that." Jail: "It was quite OK. It was a day and a night." Customs: "I still have to do interviews and explain myself for why I got arrested and why I have probation. It never goes away. So for the rest of my life, as soon as I enter American ground, I have to go to the interview. I tried to get this thing out of my file with some lawyers but it's impossible."
- Same interview: "I'm even not allowed to drink because if something happens, I go to jail forever." Fire marshals: "Everything is twice as expensive because you have to show them the whole fucking thing, the whole show in advance. You blow probably €7,000 to €10,000 a night just for the fire marshal."
- Till in "Rammstein in Amerika" (as quoted by Metal Hammer, amerika-ist-wunderbar piece): "I mean, if I get out a plastic dildo and squirt some milk around, I don't expect to be locked up because of it." Script drops the leading "I mean,".
- April 29, 2012, DCU Center Worcester: Till, "Worcester, thank you very much. I hope we don't get arrested like last time." (Louder 2022; Loudwire's version: "I hope we don't get arrested again like last time.") More than 30 semi trucks (Loudwire https://loudwire.com/rammstein-light-up-massachusetts-review-exclusive-photo-gallery/). Boston Globe (Scott McLennan, May 1, 2012): "Some bands play rock 'n' roll; Rammstein plays phallus 'n' flames." https://archive.boston.com/ae/music/articles/2012/05/01/rammstein_brings_flames_graphic_antics_to_worcester/
- About ten years away from US touring; MSG sold out Dec 11, 2010 (Metal Hammer amerika piece). Script says "after 2001"; skipped the 9/11 detail on purpose.

## Band background
- Till: born Jan 4, 1963, Leipzig; 1978 European Junior Swimming Championships; shortlisted for 1980 Moscow Olympics, left after an injury (Wikipedia, citing Die Welt). Burned ears, hair, arms early on (Wikipedia citing Blender 2007). Schneider's "Till gets burned all the time, but he likes the pain" is only via Wikipedia's Blender citation (archive blocked), so it was cut from the script.
- Till's stage fright, Rolling Stone 2014, as quoted by Metal Hammer (https://www.loudersound.com/features/the-story-of-rammstein-porno-punk-cabaret-and-pyromania): full passage used, ends "That's where the fire came into play."
- Flake: Rolling Stone Germany dossier (https://www.rollingstone.de/rammstein-alle-sechs-musiker-im-portraet-2599535/): born 1966 East Berlin; stuttered; put in dumpsters by classmates; wanted to be a surgeon; failed car salesman, "leidenschaftlicher Spaziergänger", hypochondriac; in the dinghy "wird selten zur Bühne zurückgetragen"; keyboard noise like a "sterbender Saurier" (dying dinosaur); jailed twice: "versuchter Republikflucht" because he went hiking with a friend too close to the inner-German border, and Worcester. Script states the GDR one as a fact, no take.
- Deseret News, Scott Iwasaki, June 11, 1999 (phone interview with Flake through an interpreter, the day before the arrest): "The pyrotechnics actually emerged during our second concert back in 1993... Everyone wondered where it was." "rockets, fire-drenched chain-mail, spark-spewing shoes and exploding telephones" (paper's wording). "Till had to go to school to get a certificate to handle the fireworks everyday. Otherwise we could only do the show on New Year's Eve." Family Values Salt Lake stop: "instead of a performing a simulated sex act for shock effect, Lindemann ate an apple." (The apple is the That Guy verdict.)
- Clawfinger's Zak Tell, Metal Hammer 2019 (https://www.loudersound.com/features/fireworks-flames-and-giant-uteruses-behind-the-scenes-of-rammsteins-epic-live-shows): "They had pyros, flamethrowers and in Austria, they actually set the lighting rig on fire." "Thankfully, nothing was destroyed." Rammstein supported Clawfinger, Nov 1995 to Jan 1996.
- Sept 27, 1996, "100 Years of Rammstein," Arena Berlin: "a beam loaded with fireworks fell down, injuring five people" (Metal Hammer 2019). Metal Hammer 2015 (https://www.loudersound.com/features/we-interviewed-the-man-who-sets-rammstein-on-fire) says a burning prop fell and "no-one was injured"; RammWiki says four slightly hurt plus one hospitalized. Script follows the 2019 piece, one line, said straight.
- Sabottka (FFP): Dresden school of explosives, licences, pyro factory in Montana; "We actually haven't set fire to him for the past couple tours, but we've been developing new ways of attaching flames to him." (Metal Hammer 2015)
- LeRoy Bennett (Metal Hammer 2019): the Mein Teil pot, "It would have been easier to put that thing on some kind of a lift and get it to the stage, but no, it was Till pushing it up the ramp himself... That thing is heavy, but Till's a big guy." Bloody apron and chef's hat. Bathtub with sparks. 2001 pant leg on fire, then full-body burn, Nomex suit, face mask, Flake's extinguisher of flammable powder; "I guess I better get up at the end of that, because I think they thought I died!"
- Dinghy during "Seemann"; bass player Ollie took over in 2002, per Flake because he was hurt too often (Wikipedia, Christian Lorenz). St. Petersburg 2001 tip-out is "citation needed" on Wikipedia, cut.
- Segway, Gothenburg, July 30, 2005: Flake hit Till, knee injury, Asian dates cancelled (Wikipedia citing Metal Underground, Aug 1, 2005).
- Family Values 1998: Korn, Limp Bizkit, Ice Cube, Orgy, Rammstein. Wayne Pighini (London Records) on Las Vegas Dec 1997: "Till took out the flamethrower and started blowing flame 20 feet over the crowd's heads, and the crowd was going crazy. I'd never seen anything like this". Munky in "Rammstein in Amerika": "I don't think the audience knew what hit them over the head". Jay Gordon (Orgy): "I would walk in their dressing room, and a Heineken bottle would go at 120mph past my head." Sehnsucht went platinum in the US. Lost Highway 1997. (Metal Hammer, Oct 29, 2020, amerika-ist-wunderbar piece.)
