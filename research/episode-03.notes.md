# Notes: episode-03 (Ozzy, the bat, the dove and the Alamo)

## Key facts used, with sources

### Early life
- Born John Michael Osbourne, December 1948, Aston, Birmingham. Left school around 15, bad jobs. Slaughterhouse in Digbeth, Birmingham, around 1964 (18 months to two years depending on source). Started cleaning out sheep stomachs, "for the first two or three weeks, I did nothing but throw up", then "once I'd proved myself as puke remover they promoted me to cow killer" (memoir quote as reprinted): https://www.songfacts.com/blog/writing/ozzy-and-the-abattoir , https://www.cheatsheet.com/news/ozzie-osbournes-pre-fame-job-was-gross-i-did-nothing-but-throw-up.html , https://loudwire.com/ozzy-osbourne-worst-pre-fame-jobs/
- Short jail term at Winson Green after a burglary (robbing a clothes shop), couldn't pay the fine: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozzy_Osbourne
- Music-shop card "Ozzy Zig Needs Gig" / "Requires Gig", one version adds "has own PA"; Geezer saw it; Iommi and Ward followed it up; band formed 1968: https://www.kerrang.com/black-sabbath-farewell-interview , https://www.birminghamdispatch.co.uk/how-1960s-industrial-brum-shaped-black-sabbath/ , https://www.guitarworld.com/artists/guitarists/gibson-tv-tony-iommi-documentary , https://ultimateclassicrock.com/geezer-butler-ozzy-osbourne-first-meet/
- Fired from Sabbath April 1979; Dio replaced him; Sharon Arden (daughter of Sabbath manager Don Arden) managed him; Randy Rhoads hired: https://ultimateclassicrock.com/ozzy-osbourne-fired-from-black-sabbath/ , https://www.musicradar.com/artists/it-wasnt-just-my-decision-to-fire-ozzy-it-was-a-band-decision-it-was-either-we-break-up-or-we-carry-on-without-him-tony-iommi-says-they-had-to-get-rid-of-ozzy-osbourne-to-make-black-sabbath-great-again

### The dove
- March 27, 1981 (the US release date of Blizzard of Ozz), CBS sales convention in Los Angeles; "words and photos emerged" that he'd bitten the heads off two white doves. Mick Wall: Ozzy polished off a bottle of brandy in the car on the way. Ozzy to Wall: bit the first one "just to shut [her] up" (a PR woman), then did the second, "That's when they threw me out." Later to Sounds: "The bird was dead... we were planning to release it there, but it died beforehand. So rather than waste it I bit its head off... You should have seen their faces." https://www.mojo4music.com/time-machine/1980s/mojo-time-machine-ozzy-osbourne-bites-the-head-off-a-dove/ , https://www.pressreader.com/uk/mojo-uk/20210401/283046942173118 , https://www.tvguide.co.uk/articles/im-a-celebrity-jack-osbournes-dad-ozzy-osbourne-dove-story
- Sharon's idea (some say three doves); Billboard says half a bottle of whiskey; Billboard, citing Sharon's 2013 book: horrified executives had them escorted out and banned from returning: https://www.billboard.com/lists/ozzy-osbourne-controversies-timeline/
- One retelling of his recollection: spat the head out on the table, the woman fell on the floor screaming: https://www.iheart.com/content/2018-03-27-that-time-ozzy-bit-the-heads-off-two-doves/ , https://bestclassicbands.com/?p=14837
- "It was not a publicity stunt, I was just out of my f*cking face on drugs and alcohol": https://heavy.com/entertainment/2020/09/ozzy-osbourne-bit-doves-heads-off
- Rudy Sarzo: a short series of photos of the PR executive's reaction; he joined the band that day, met Ozzy at the Arden property, and Ozzy had forgotten another dove in his jacket and bit its head off too; Sharon always "right behind him to spin it": https://news.yahoo.com/bassist-rudy-sarzo-remembers-ozzy-osbournes-bat-biting-40-years-later-185555775.html , https://ultimateclassicrock.com/ozzy-osbourne-bit-another-dove-head-off/
- Label: CBS (Epic per some sources). Location LA in most sources, New York in Guitar World: https://www.guitarworld.com/features/ozzy-osbourne-hell-and-back , https://au.rollingstone.com/music/music-lists/ozzy-osbourne-wildest-most-beloved-pop-culture-moments-list-80483/ozzy-has-a-very-unfortunate-meeting-with-a-couple-of-doves-80495
- Blizzard of Ozz certified 5x platinum in the US (Feb 2019): https://blabbermouth.net/news/ozzy-osbournes-crazy-train-surpasses-one-billion-streams-on-spotify

### The kid and the bat
- Wednesday, January 20, 1982, Veterans Memorial Auditorium, Des Moines: https://www.upi.com/Archives/1982/01/21/Diagnosis-Patient-bit-head-off-bat/5450380437200/
- Mark Neal, 17, high school student, named in the Des Moines Register two days after the show (Jan 22, 1982, reporter Nick Lamberto). Quotes: "It really freaked me out." "I won't get in any trouble for admitting this, will I?" Neal said he threw the dead bat, saw Ozzy pick it up, bite its head off and throw it into the audience: https://www.yahoo.com/now/archives-ozzy-osbourne-leaves-des-131737421.html , https://ultimateclassicrock.com/ozzy-osbourne-bat-biting-mark-neal/ , https://loudwire.com/ozzy-osbourne-bat-biting-mark-neal/
- Two weeks before the show his YOUNGER brother came home from school with the bat, planned to keep it as a pet, it died. Friend Carmen (Martin-)Kelly persuaded him to freeze it. Carmen: "We were telling everybody before the concert that we were bringing a bat." A later Register article says the bat was already dead when the brother found it: https://loudwire.com/ozzy-osbourne-bat-incident-story-you-didnt-know/ , https://ultimateclassicrock.com/ozzy-osbourne-bat-biting-mark-neal/ , https://americansongwriter.com/everything-history-got-wrong-about-ozzy-osbournes-infamous-bat-incident/
- Sealed in a plastic bag to sneak it in: https://loudwire.com/ozzy-osbourne-bat-biting-mark-neal/
- Neal: "It landed in front of Rudy Sarzo, the bass player. He looked down at it and motioned to Ozzy and, as they say, the rest is history." Sarzo: pointed at it with his bass headstock and kept playing; ambulance waiting after; Sharon told him "He bit into a bat": https://www.loudersound.com/news/ozzy-osbourne-bassist-rudy-sarzo-remembers-bat-biting-2025
- Rolling Stone's 2022 40th-anniversary piece said the thrower never came forward; American Songwriter and the Register point out that's false: https://americansongwriter.com/everything-history-got-wrong-about-ozzy-osbournes-infamous-bat-incident/

## Settled from the last fact-check pass
- "Younger brother": confirmed by the Register as reported by Loudwire/UCR. "Planning to keep it" also confirmed (as a pet).
- "They kept him" after the dove: wrong. They threw him out (Ozzy) and banned him from coming back (Sharon via Billboard). The record still came out on the label and sold millions. Script now says exactly that.
- "Dove already dead": sourced. Ozzy told Sounds it died beforehand (via Mojo).
- "Mouth full of something warm": sourced. I Am Ozzy, "warm, gloopy liquid", then the head twitched.
- City bans: removed. Real aftermath used instead: Vets Auditorium rule, mayor's question, Milwaukee humane officer.
- Alamo: script now says the charge was public intoxication and the spot was the Cenotaph.
- Mark Neal "came forward years later": wrong. He was named in the Register two days after the show (1982); the Register retold it in 2005 and 2018. Script says "two days later".
- Rabies: Register says he carried a four-day vaccine supply; UPI says he'd check in with hospitals on the route. Script uses the Register's line.

## Quote bank: Des Moines Register, Jan 22, 1982 (full text, verbatim)
Source: Nick Lamberto, Des Moines Register, Jan 22, 1982, reprinted as "From the archives: Ozzy Osbourne leaves Des Moines with 'bat taste' in mouth": https://www.yahoo.com/now/archives-ozzy-osbourne-leaves-des-131737421.html (fetched Nov 17, 2026, full network access). Not yet used in the script; Duck approves any additions first.

> Rock Singer Ozzy Osbourne left Des Moines Thursday with a $39,000 haul from a concert at Veterans Memorial Auditorium, a four-day supply of rabies vaccine and a bat taste in his mouth.
> Osbourne reportedly put a dead bat in his mouth, bit its head off and threw it back into the crowd of about 5,000 at the auditorium Wednesday night.
> Some skeptics think the whole thing was a publicity stunt – even the taking of the first of a series of five rabies shots at a Des Moines hospital after the concert.
> But Mark Neal, 17, of Des Moines said he threw the dead bat onto the stage, saw Osbourne pick it up, bite its head off and then throw it into the audience.
> "It really freaked me out," Neal said. "I won't get in any trouble for admitting this, will I?"
> After the show, Osbourne went to Mercy Hospital Medical Center, and was referred to Broadlawns Medical Center because rabies vaccine was available there.
> Osbourne, listed as John M. Osbourne, 33, of Beverly Hills, California, was given a tetanus shot and a rabies shot and took enough rabies vaccine with him to complete the series, according to Winifred Barden, a supervisor at Broadlawns.
> Pam Culver, another Broadlawns supervisor, said Osbourne paid $500 to $600 in cash for the medical care and vaccine.
> "If it was a joke or publicity stunt, he sure put himself to a lot of trouble to do it," Culver said.
> Des Moines Mayor Pete Crivaro said he wants "to find out if the performer violated an agreement on the use of animals in his act. We had been assured he wasn't going to, and I want to find out who is lying."
> Dave Palmitier, the manager of the auditorium, said Osbourne "did let some doves loose, which he wasn't supposed to do. We found one dead that apparently had been killed in the audience."
> Des Moines Police Lt. Derald Leaming said he had warned Osbourne's manager before the show that if an animal was harmed, he and Osbourne "were going to jail because it's against Iowa law.
> "We had heard about Osbourne sticking birds in his mouth and warned them he would be arrested on the spot if he bit them."
> Leaming said he didn't see the bat incident.
> Bruce Kapp, an executive for Contemporary Productions of St. Louis, Missouri, which booked the Wednesday show, said Osbourne put a dead bat in his mouth, "but I don't know if he bit the head off …"
> Kapp said police and auditorium officials had "told us not to do anything with live animals and we agreed not to. He did release seven doves to the audience, which he does every night."
> He said Osbourne closes his act "by putting raw liver in his mouth and spitting it out, and people think he bit something in two. There's no truth to the report that he bit the head off a chicken in Omaha."
> Osbourne is scheduled to perform Saturday night at the Dane County Coliseum at Madison, Wisconsin. He could not be reached for comment Thursday.

Note: the Register's own dash in "publicity stunt – even" is the paper's punctuation; if read on air it's just a pause (no dash in the script).
