# Notes: story-disco-demolition

Research method: WebSearch only. WebFetch was blocked for every outside host in this session (wikipedia.org, wbur.org returned ENOTFOUND), so facts were cross-checked across search-result summaries from multiple sources.

## Key facts used, with sources
- Date July 12, 1979, Comiskey Park, White Sox vs Tigers twi-night doubleheader. Crate of disco records blown up in center field between games: https://www.britannica.com/event/Disco-Demolition-Night , https://www.mlb.com/news/disco-demolition-night-chicago-comiskey-park
- 98 cents admission with a disco record, a nod to WLUP 97.9 ("The Loop"): https://www.cbsnews.com/chicago/news/disco-demolition-steve-dahl/ , https://www.npr.org/transcripts/485873750
- Official attendance 47,795 vs capacity about 44,500. Higher crowd estimates, about 20,000 more outside: https://www.baseball-almanac.com/box-scores/boxscore.php?boxid=197907121CHA , https://www.mentalfloss.com/article/609062/disco-demolition-night-comiskey-park
- Rusty Staub to the NYT: "People brought ladders": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Veeck (quoted via search summary)
- Security mostly at the gates, field unguarded: https://www.mentalfloss.com/article/609062/disco-demolition-night-comiskey-park
- Tigers won game one 4-1: https://www.baseball-almanac.com/box-scores/boxscore.php?boxid=197907121CHA
- Dahl in army fatigues and helmet; he and Garry Meier circled the field in a Jeep; crowd threw firecrackers and beer; "Disco sucks" chant: https://www.chicagohistory.org/disco-demolition-night/ , https://baseballhistory.org/articles/disco-demolition-night-comiskey-park
- Field rush ("up to 7,000"), seats torn out, bonfires in outfield: https://www.mlb.com/news/disco-demolition-night-chicago-comiskey-park , https://sabr.org/gamesproj/game/july-12-1979-chicagos-disco-demolition-night-doubleheader-results-in-loss-and-forfeit/
- Veeck, Dahl and Harry Caray pleaded over PA; "Take Me Out to the Ball Game" played (sources differ on who started it); riot police arrived, applauded by remaining fans: https://www.mentalfloss.com/sports/baseball/disco-demolition-night-comiskey-park , https://www.mlb.com/news/disco-demolition-night-chicago-comiskey-park
- 39 arrests (some reports 37), mostly disorderly conduct: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disco_Demolition_Night
- Sparky Anderson refused to field his team, safety; Lee MacPhail forfeited game to Detroit 9-0; first MLB forfeit in five years: https://baseballhistory.org/articles/disco-demolition-night-comiskey-park , https://sabr.org/gamesproj/game/july-12-1979-chicagos-disco-demolition-night-doubleheader-results-in-loss-and-forfeit/
- "Last American League forfeit" (trivia framing): https://www.plansponsor.com/?p=71788 (title only; phrased in script as "what the trivia books call")
- Dahl: came from Detroit radio, WDAI doubled his salary, started Feb 1978, fired Christmas Eve 1978 when WDAI went disco, called it "Disco D.I.E.", parody songs, "Insane Coho Lips" army, age 24: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Dahl , https://news.wttw.com/2016/07/11/steve-dahl-dissects-disco-demolition
- Dahl: disco "probably on its way out", night "hastened its demise"; career took off: https://www.lib.niu.edu/2004/ih040506.html , https://www.dnainfo.com/chicago/20160801/armour-square/steve-dahl-on-disco-demolition-night-that-really-began-his-career
- Bill Veeck: Marine, leg crushed by recoiling gun (likely Bougainville), about 36 surgeries, amputated, cut ashtray into wooden leg; father was Cubs president: https://www.newworldencyclopedia.org/entry/Bill_Veeck , https://case.edu/ech/articles/v/veeck-william-bill-l
- Eddie Gaedel 1951 (3'7", number 1/8, walked on four pitches), exploding scoreboard at Comiskey, names on jerseys, Sox in shorts: https://baseballhall.org/hall-of-famers/veeck-bill , https://www.espn.com/classic/veeckbill000816.html , https://www.mlb.com/cut4/larry-doby-eddie-gaedel-and-disco-demolition-night-the-legend-of-bill-veeck/c-40836078
- Mike Veeck was promotions director; left Sox late 1980; about 20 years before next big-league job (Tampa Bay Devil Rays); co-owns St. Paul Saints, Bill Murray a co-owner; said Disco Demolition "made me great at what I do": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Veeck , https://news.wttw.com/sites/default/files/article/file-attachments/DiscoDemolition_Chapter1.pdf , https://www.mlb.com/news/mike-veeck-documentary-details-his-minor-league-baseball-impact
- Vince Lawrence: teen usher (14 or 15), saving for a synthesizer, says records brought included Tyrone Davis, Curtis Mayfield, Otis Clay (Black artists, not disco); later co-wrote house track "On and On". Dahl denies racist/homophobic intent: https://www.npr.org/transcripts/485873750 , https://www.chicagoist.com/2016/07/12/disco_demolition.php , https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/vince-lawrence-disco-demolition-night-move-ya-body-clip-1235364056/
