# Fact-check notes: story-milli-vanilli

Research note: only web search result summaries were reachable in this session (direct page fetches were blocked). Facts below were confirmed in search-returned excerpts.

## Key facts used, with sources
- Lake Compounce, Bristol CT, July 21 1989, Club MTV tour; track looped on "Girl, you know it's..."; Rob ran off stage; Rob quote to MTV ("couldn't repeat it 15 times... panicked... went off stage") paraphrased; "beginning of the end": https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/lake-commence-89-the-disastrous-show-that-exposed-milli-vanilli/ , https://www.parkrovers.com/home/2021/7/21/milli-vanillis-lip-sync-scandal-at-lake-compounce , https://www.biography.com/musicians/milli-vanilli-lip-sync-scandal
- Frank Farian created Boney M; Farian sang the male vocals in the studio, Bobby Farrell performed on TV (not a secret): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boney_M. , https://www.metafilter.com/88346/The-Secret-History-Of-Frank-Farian
- Rob Pilatus: German dancer mother, African American serviceman father; orphanage in Oberammergau first four years; adopted by Munich family; bullied as a child: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Pilatus
- Fab Morvan from Paris, moved to Germany at 18; met Rob in Munich (accounts differ: dance seminar/club in Munich vs. LA then Munich): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fab_Morvan , https://www.salon.com/2023/10/27/milli-vanilli-documentary-paramount/
- Studio singers incl. Charles Shaw (rapper), Brad Howell (lead), John Davis; duo signed without legal advice: https://www.salon.com/2023/10/27/milli-vanilli-documentary-paramount/ , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milli_Vanilli
- "Girl You Know It's True" No. 2 Hot 100; three No. 1s ("Baby Don't Forget My Number", "Girl I'm Gonna Miss You", "Blame It on the Rain"); every single top 5; album 6x platinum Jan 1990: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girl_You_Know_It%27s_True_(album) , https://stereogum.com/?p=2158368
- Time magazine quote ("Musically, we are more talented than any Bob Dylan... than Paul McCartney. Mick Jagger, his lines are not clear."), Rob age 24: https://time.com/archive/6714303/music-two-scoops-of-vanilli
- Grammy Best New Artist Feb 21 1990: https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/lip-synching-duo-milli-vanilli-lose-grammy-award/
- Charles Shaw told Newsday (Dec 1989), then retracted: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Shaw_(singer) , https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2019/09/04/retrospective-30-years-since-milli-vanilli/
- Farian admission Nov 1990 (dates vary 12 to 15 Nov, so script says "November 1990"); Grammy revoked days later (19 or 20 Nov), the only Grammy ever revoked; duo reportedly offered to return it and asked it go to Davis, Howell, Shaw; LA press conference with 200+ reporters, sang a cappella: https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/music/lip-synching-duo-milli-vanilli-lose-grammy-award/ , https://popcrush.com/milli-vanilli-grammy-revoked/
- Refunds: Chicago settlement, ~10 million eligible buyers, ~$1 single / $2 cassette or LP / $3 CD, kept the records; Arista dropped them and deleted the album: https://pmamagazine.org/girl-you-know-its-false-the-milli-vanilli-lip-sync-scandal/ , https://www.grunge.com/122943/whatever-happened-milli-vanilli/
- Fab owns the name, sings live; 2023 Paramount+ documentary; memoir "You Know It's True" audiobook nominated 2026 Grammy (Best Audio Book, Narration & Storytelling), lost to the Dalai Lama: https://www.npr.org/2025/11/20/nx-s1-5612291/milli-vanillis-fab-morvan-gets-a-surprising-grammy-nomination , https://www.businesstoday.in/trending/entertainment/story/dalai-lama-wins-grammy-for-audiobook-beats-trevor-noah-and-ketanji-brown-jackson-514120-2026-02-02
- Farian died Jan 2024 at home in Miami, aged 82; later acts La Bouche, No Mercy: https://thewrap.com/frank-farian-milli-vanilli-mastermind-dies-obit
- Rob Pilatus died April 1998 in a hotel near Frankfurt (Friedrichsdorf), alcohol and prescription drugs, ruled accidental: https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/milli-vanillis-rob-pilatus-dead-at-33-2-243519/ , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Pilatus

## "Story goes" / attributed pile, and why
- Name from a Turkish slogan: only loosely sourced, framed "the story goes".
- Downtown Julie Brown getting Rob back on stage: her own account (Behind the Music), framed "according to her".
- Farian paying Shaw $150,000 to retract: widely repeated with "allegedly", no primary proof; framed "reportedly".
- Why Farian confessed (duo wanted to sing on next album): presented as "the usual version".
- Duo offering to return the Grammy: "reportedly".
