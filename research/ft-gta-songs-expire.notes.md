# Fresh Tracks Short: GTA songs have an expiration date (Nov 24, 2026)

Duck's pitch: "enjoy the songs on there while u can, because the songs may not always be in the game... then tell why and what has happened." Tied to the GTA 6 launch (Nov 19/20, 2026; companion album Nov 20, deluxe Nov 23, Consequence/Vice).
- Rockstar licenses music for a term (about ten years); when it ends, songs are removed by update.
- Vice City, Nov 2012: pulled from Steam over a licensing claim tied to Michael Jackson's "Wanna Be Startin' Somethin'" (Sony); came back with nearly 10 songs removed (PC Gamer, CinemaBlend). The 2021 Definitive Edition doesn't have "Billie Jean" (GINX and others).
- San Andreas, 2014: an update removed 17 songs from Steam users' purchased copies (Techdirt).
- GTA IV: released April 29, 2008; update April 26, 2018 removed songs as licenses expired, with some replacements (Rockstar statement to USgamer; Game Informer). Count around 50 (a forum count says 54), so the script says "dozens." Gamepur headline: the soundtrack "Just Lost David Bowie and AC/DC." The Doors and Stevie Nicks appear in some summaries but weren't verified, so they're left out.
- No artist lawsuits found over the removals; it's contract expiry, not a fight.
