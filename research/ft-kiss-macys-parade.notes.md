# Fresh Track: KISS at the 2014 Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade (Thanksgiving throwback, Nov 26, 2026)
Duck (Nov 26): "it could work today cuz it's thanksgiving, that's what makes it relevant now." Prompted by Ultimate Classic Rock's yearly repost.

- Parade: Thursday, Nov 27, 2014, the 88th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, NBC (Blabbermouth, Loudwire). Blabbermouth: a "rocking KISS medley" led by 600+ cheerleaders; the song and whether it was live are not stated anywhere checked, so the script doesn't say.
- Paul Stanley right after (Twitter, per UCR and Far Out): "screwed over and misled"; "we ALL deserved better".
- Paul Stanley, Nov 2020 tweet (per UCR): shown sketches of a HUGE float and told they'd be surrounded by "100 baton twirlers in Kiss makeup"; "Instead we got an SUV with a U-Haul attached and two unimpressed escorts."
- Doc McGhee, Q&A on the ninth KISS Kruise (per UCR, "five years later", about 2019): "The worst thing we did was we played the Macy's Day Parade"; the planned float was the Gibson float; the night before they learned Paul was supposed to play Gibson but plays Washburn; the replacement "is literally like, 25 feet long with two microphones on it."; "ohhhh, this is how you get fired." He wasn't fired and still manages KISS.
- Caution: UCR and Far Out carry the same McGhee quotes (not independent). The pre-parade press (Loudwire) announced cheerleaders, not baton twirlers; the twirlers are Paul's claim, so the script attributes them to him.
