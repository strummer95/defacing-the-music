## "Story goes" / "papers reported" pile, and why
- Lorry driver (47, eight-year-old son, kicked in new TV, "blew up and I was knocked backwards"): sourced only to tabloid coverage (Daily Mirror) in the middle of a tabloid frenzy; name given variously as James/Jason Holmes in retellings, so the script does not name him and frames it as "the papers reported".
- Freddie "hadn't seen a dentist in 15 years": "the story goes"; the dental emergency itself is in every account.
- "I Hate Pink Floyd" spotting: "the most repeated version".
- Jones's "we've fucking spent it": exists in some transcripts but not all; paraphrased as "depending on the transcript".
- Lydon vs. others on who put on the jukebox song: both versions given.

## Exact-quote policy
- Transcripts differ slightly (Guardian 2004 transcript could not be fetched). Only short lines that appear consistently across multiple transcripts are quoted verbatim; the rest is paraphrased, and the script says so on air.
