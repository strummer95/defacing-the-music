### 1969

## "Story goes" pile
- Organizers asking Fred to calm the crowd before the set: never proven; script calls it a legend. Some write-ups (Loudwire, Mic, SFGate) quote Fred on stage: "They wanna ask us to ask you to mellow out a little bit. They say too many people are getting hurt." The script says a couple of write-ups have Fred himself saying "they" wanted it, and that he was mocking it.
- Organizers' claim that on-site firefighters wouldn't leave their station: organizer claim, framed as "their version, nobody's proven it."
- Who lit the first fire: not established. Script says candles, bottles and plywood all fed the fires.
