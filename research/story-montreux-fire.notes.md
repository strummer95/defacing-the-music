# story-montreux-fire: fact-check notes

Research note: this session could not fetch pages directly (wikipedia.org, npr.org, montreuxjazzfestival.com were blocked by the network allowlist), so every fact below was checked through web-search result summaries of the listed pages. Worth a spot check by a human before recording.

## Key facts used
- Fire on Dec 4, 1971 (a Saturday) at the Montreux Casino during Frank Zappa and the Mothers' show, during "King Kong," in Don Preston's Minimoog solo; casino and all the Mothers' gear destroyed. https://ultimateclassicrock.com/frank-zappa-fire-smoke-on-the-water/ ; https://www.montreuxjazzfestival.com/en/news/smoke-on-the-water-50-years/ ; https://www.progressiveears.org/forum/archive/index.php/t-28144.html
- Afternoon / matinee show. Montreux Jazz Festival 50th piece (summary gave ~4.15pm) and Machine Head article ("matinée"). https://www.montreuxjazzfestival.com/en/news/smoke-on-the-water-50-years/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_Head_(album)
- About 2,000 people; Zappa calmly led the evacuation; exits were a small front door or a plate glass window by the stage. https://americansongwriter.com/on-this-day-in-1971-a-fan-fired-flare-gun-ended-a-frank-zappa-concert-early-and-inspired-this-classic-rock-hit ; https://www.alainrieder.com/en/tmblog/?post=frank-zappa-montreux-1971
- No deaths; injuries described as minor. https://americansongwriter.com/on-this-day-in-1971-a-fan-fired-flare-gun-ended-a-frank-zappa-concert-early-and-inspired-this-classic-rock-hit (single, lighter source; phrased as "as far as every account I could find")
- Claude Nobs: born 1936, apprenticed as a cook, worked at the Montreux tourism office, began staging concerts mid-60s, founded the Jazz Festival in 1967, ran it for decades; saved young people who had hidden in the casino; "Funky Claude" line; died Jan 2013. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Nobs ; https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/culture/founder-of-montreux-jazz-festival-dies/34699960 ; https://www.claudenobsfoundation.com/bio-cn
- Zappa born Baltimore 1940, father a defense-industry chemist; Zappa said he learned to make gunpowder around age six, experimented until about 15. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Zappa ; https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/the-unusual-childhood-of-frank-zappa/
- Freak Out! 1966 (common knowledge, Wikipedia FZ page).
- Mark Volman and Howard Kaylan (ex-Turtles, "Flo and Eddie") joined in 1970, stayed until Dec 1971; sang "Happy Together" on Fillmore East June 1971. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Kaylan ; https://wiki.killuglyradio.com/wiki/Flo
- Arthur Brown joke, attribution disputed (Zappa / Volman / Kaylan); eyewitness Alain Rieder calls the "Zappa laughed it off" version absurd. https://www.afka.net/Articles/1972-01_Rock_&_Folk.htm ; https://www.alainrieder.com/en/tmblog/?post=frank-zappa-montreux-1971
- Deep Purple (Gillan, Blackmore, Lord, Glover, Paice) booked the casino off-season with the Rolling Stones Mobile to record; got passes to the Zappa show in their hotel rooms (Glover); Gillan was in the audience. https://www.songfacts.com/facts/deep-purple/smoke-on-the-water ; https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/frank-zappa-deep-purple-50-years-on-from-the-montreux-casino-fire/ ; https://www.loudersound.com/features/deep-purple-machine-head-ian-gillan-interview
- Stones Mobile used on parts of Led Zeppelin IV: well documented general knowledge (Headley Grange sessions); not separately searched.
- Title credited to Roger Glover; two versions (dream days later vs written down at once). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smoke_on_the_Water ; https://www.loudersound.com/features/the-story-behind-deep-purple-smoke-on-the-water
- Riff born in a jam at the Pavilion; police came after neighbor complaints and the crew held the door shut to finish the take (Glover, Paice). https://guitarworld.com/news/deep-purple-smoke-on-the-water-police ; https://musicradar.com/rhythm/ian-paice-cops-pounded-the-door-as-we-recorded-smoke-on-the-water-557793
- Then the empty Grand Hotel, Stones truck out back, cables through windows; lyrics mostly Gillan; "some stupid with a flare gun" lyric. https://www.songfacts.com/facts/deep-purple/smoke-on-the-water
- Riff in fourths (Blackmore, Glover). https://www.guitarworld.com/features/ritchie-blackmore-smoke-on-the-water-riff
- Gillan: "just another riff," not seen as a single. https://www.guitarplayer.com/music/albums-singles/deep-purple-on-the-writing-and-legacy-of-smoke-on-the-water
- Machine Head released March 1972; single 1973, #4 Billboard Hot 100. https://parade.com/entertainment/1972-rock-anthem-deep-purple-smoke-water-inspired-catastrophic-concert-fire-timeless-classic ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_Head_(album)
- Casino rebuilt, reopened 1975 with Mountain Studios; Queen bought it in 1979; Freddie statue on lakefront. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen:_The_Studio_Experience ; https://www.redsharknews.com/audio/item/4176-studio-profile-mountain-studios (statue: general knowledge, not separately confirmed in a search result)
- Rainbow Theatre, Dec 10, 1971: Trevor Howell pushed Zappa into the orchestra pit (10-15 ft, sources differ); broken leg, head injury, back/neck injuries; long wheelchair period; "the band thought I was dead"; voice permanently dropped (Zappa: "a third"); Howell got a year. https://wiki.killuglyradio.com/wiki/The_Rainbow_Theatre_Incident ; https://www.loudersound.com/features/frank-zappa-onstage-injury-london-1972 ; https://ultimateclassicrock.com/41-years-ago-frank-zappa-pushed-off-stage-in-london/

## "The story goes" pile
- The Arthur Brown "Fire!" joke: who said it, and whether anyone did, is disputed.
- Flare gun vs rocket/Roman candle: Zappa himself described it differently.
- Blackmore's "Beethoven's Fifth backwards" claim: his own words, but widely doubted.
- Trevor Howell's motive: girlfriend story vs "bad value for money" (The Times); both given, framed as disputed.

## Checked and left out
- The flare-gun man's name: Wikipedia names a Swiss police suspect, but I could not find solid enough sourcing to name him on air. Script says a name "floats around online" without saying it.
- "Zappa's show was the last of the season": partly supported (Glover says casino closed for winter, show was the last before Purple's booking). Script says the casino's last show of the year was Zappa; mild risk.
- "Armed police" at the Pavilion: not supported, left out. Exact time of the police visit (one account says ~2am): script says "middle of the night."
- The value of the Mothers' lost gear: no source found.
- Mattresses on the hotel walls: unverified, removed.
