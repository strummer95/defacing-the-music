# Notes: story-dmb-bus

Research method: WebSearch only (WebFetch blocked for outside hosts in this session).

## Key facts used, with sources
- August 8, 2004 (a Sunday). Bus emptied its blackwater tank through the open metal grate of the Kinzie Street bridge onto an open-top sightseeing boat: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Matthews_Band_bus_incident , https://blockclubchicago.org/2019/08/08/dave-matthews-tour-bus-dumped-800-pounds-of-poop-on-people-taking-a-chicago-river-cruise-15-years-ago-today/
- Boat: Chicago's Little Lady. More than 100 passengers (sources give about 100 to 120, one says 109): https://www.cbsnews.com/chicago/news/dave-matthews-tour-bus-chicago-river-anniversary/ , https://www.9news.com/article/entertainment/dave-matthews-band-bus-driver-charged-in-waste-dumping-incident/73-344806425
- About 800 lbs, and Billboard's "80 to 100 gallons": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Matthews_Band_bus_incident , https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/dave-matthews-band-settles-bus-waste-case-63070/
- Tribune reporter on board; "brownish-yellow slurry", ruined clothes, several made sick: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Matthews_Band_bus_incident
- Docent: "some water has hit the deck"; man in back: "That's not water, buddy"; screaming; $25 refunds: https://www.chicagomag.com/city-life/thats-not-water-buddy-an-oral-history-of-poopgate/ (via search snippets)
- Wohl drove Boyd Tinsley's bus, alone, heading to the Peninsula Hotel; band in town for two nights at Alpine Valley, East Troy WI: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Matthews_Band_bus_incident , https://do312.com/p/do312-explains-poopgate
- AG suit says driver "flicked a switch behind the seat": https://www.9news.com/article/entertainment/dave-matthews-band-bus-driver-charged-in-waste-dumping-incident/73-344806425
- Band publicist said all buses were parked; Wohl initially denied it: https://www.cbsnews.com/chicago/news/dave-matthews-tour-bus-chicago-river-anniversary/
- East Bank Club security video identified the bus: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Matthews_Band_bus_incident
- Charged Jan 2005 by Cook County SA Dick Devine (reckless conduct, discharging contaminants), turned himself in; pleaded guilty March 2005; $10,000 max fine to Friends of the Chicago River, 18 months probation, 150 hours community service; fired: https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/dmb-bus-driver-charged-in-river-dump-110076/ , https://news.pollstar.com/2005/03/10/dmb-driver-pleads-guilty/
- Lisa Madigan suit; $200,000 settlement April 2005, no admission of guilt, written tank procedures and a log through April 2010; $50k each to Park District and Friends of the Chicago River; separate personal-injury suits; city also sued: https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/dave-matthews-band-settles-bus-waste-case-63070/ , https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2005/apr/30/band-settles-waste-dumping-suit/
- Band statement ("environmentalism, accountability, and, mostly, principles of humanity"); Dave in 2009 would apologize "as long as I have to": https://ultimateclassicrock.com/dave-matthews-poopgate/
- BamaGreen launched 2004 (biodiesel, plastic reduction); UNEP Goodwill Ambassador 2019: https://consequence.net/2019/06/dave-matthews-band-un-goodwill-ambassador/ , https://variety.com/2019/music/features/dave-matthews-environment-touring-united-nations-1203328977/
- Band background: Matthews emigrated from South Africa, settled in Charlottesville 1986, bartended at Miller's; band formed 1991; first public show Charlottesville Earth Day Festival April 1991; Tinsley full-time 1992; top-grossing touring band of 2000, $68.2M: https://americansongwriter.com/the-jam-band-origins-of-the-dave-matthews-band , https://www.allmusic.com/artist/dave-matthews-mn0000687561/biography
- Rock and Roll Hall of Fame 2024: https://rockhall.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/2024-Induction-Ceremony-Program-Essay-Dave-Matthews-Band.pdf
- Kinzie Street bridge: bascule over North Branch, open steel grate deck, about 1909: https://www.chicagoloopbridges.com/bridges12/NB16/Kinzie.html
- Chicago River reversed in 1900 to send sewage away from the lake/drinking water; dyed green on St. Patrick's Day: general Chicago history, not separately searched (very well established).
