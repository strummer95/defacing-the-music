- August 8, 2004 (a Sunday). ### Decisions chain (pillar 2) as written
Wohl reaches back and flips the switch on the bridge (engineers: not an accident) -> tells investigators he drove over but nothing came out -> band trusts its driver: publicist "all buses parked", letter says driver suspended and denying, DNA offer -> cameras -> charges, plea, firing -> band pays, logbook.
