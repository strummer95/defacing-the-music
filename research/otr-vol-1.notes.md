# Off The Record: Candy, Blood and a Cow Tongue (research notes, Nov 23, 2026)

Sources list: lib/sources/otr-vol-1.json. Researched in parallel; caveats below matter before recording.

## Van Halen's brown M&M's
- Rider line (1982 tour, "Munchies"): "M & M's (WARNING: ABSOLUTELY NO BROWN ONES)" (Snopes, Mental Floss). Same rider: herring in sour cream, Blue Nun "to be served at room temperature."
- "Article 126" and its legal wording are from Roth's memoir Crazy from the Heat (1997, pp. 97-98), not the rider document. Script quotes the memoir as Roth's telling.
- Nine 18-wheelers where three was normal: Roth's memoir via Snopes.
- Pueblo: March 30, 1980, Massari Gym, University of Southern Colorado; tickets $8.50 ($7.50 students); 5,000 people (Pueblo Chieftain, March 28, 2010).
- Damage figures conflict: Roth says $12,000 backstage and $80,000 floor; Chieftain says dinner rooms about $3,500 and floor estimates "as high as $80,000," band reps said closer to $12,000; a student crew member saw "one or two impressions." Script uses only Roth's $12,000 as his own words.
- Quotes (exact via Snopes/Chieftain): Roth "So, when I would walk backstage..."; "I went into full Shakespearean..."; "Well, who am I to get in the way of a good rumor?"; VP William Provance "ate the lasagna with their hands, threw the food around the room, smeared the food on the walls and on each other."
- Prices: CPI-U annual averages 1980 = 82.4, 2025 about 322 (factor about 3.9). $8.50 is about $33; $12,000 is about $47,000. Recheck with the BLS CPI calculator before recording.

## KISS blood comic
- Blood drawn by a registered nurse backstage at Nassau Coliseum, Feb 21, 1977; refrigerated under guard; flown to the Borden Ink plant, Depew (Buffalo), NY; poured May 1977 (25th or 26th disputed). Notary: name unknown; Simmons says the notary witnessed the drawing, other sources say the pour.
- Contract fragment (exact via Far Out): "This is to certify that Kiss members Gene Simmons, Ace Frehley, Paul Stanley, and Peter Criss have each donated blood".
- Cover: "Printed in real KISS blood." $1.50 vs 30 cents; about 900,000 printed, roughly half a million sold (Comics Journal #45 via Wikipedia). 1977 CPI 60.6, so $1.50 is about $8.
- Stan Lee (Web of Stories, exact): "You guys are out of your mind." "Publicity is really one of the most important things in the world." "some publicity guy got the idea."
- Simmons (exact, via KISS Timeline / Far Out): "someone came up with the idea of putting real blood in the ink"; "wasn't me".
- Cherry bomb under Stan Lee's seat: artist Alan Weiss's memory (Intelligent Collector).
- Sports Illustrated mix-up: rumor only (Snopes, Far Out). Script frames it as "the story goes."
- Do NOT claim it was Marvel's best-seller until 1990: treated as legend.

## Gene Simmons's tongue
- Bus-debate quote is from Lynn Saxberg, Ottawa Citizen, Sept 21, 2000, as quoted by Snopes (secondhand; paywalled). "whether Gene Simmons really had a cow tongue grafted to his own tongue."
- Beef tongue about 3 lb (LA Times 1989); human tongue about 2.5 oz.
- Memoir Kiss and Make-Up (2001) quote via Grunge/Suggest, not checked against the book: "It really was longer than everyone else's."
- Blood idea (exact, Kiss: Behind the Mask p.153 via KISS Timeline): "I thought, that's cool, I should do that onstage."
- Hair on fire first Dec 31, 1973, Academy of Music NYC, opening for Blue Oyster Cult; "probably six or seven times" (1999 chat, UCR).
- $1 million insurance and 7 inches: unverified, said as legends. Guinness longest tongue 3.97 in (Nick Stoeberl).
- Genie the calf, Kerrville, Texas, 2017 (Time).
- Not used: Daily Beast "cow's tongue" quote (unverified, paywalled).
