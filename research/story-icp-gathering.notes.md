# story-icp-gathering: fact-check notes (Nov 27, 2026, new script)

Research via WebSearch/WebFetch. Quotes in the script were copied from the pages below (fetch tool returned exact sentences; long passages were checked in segments). Behind the Paint itself was not readable online; ICP's own accounts come from the Spin 2012 oral history instead. Spot check before recording.

## Care choices (Duck: "an episode to be careful with")
- Fans are never the joke; the joke is the booking decision and the chain of venues saying no. Rock throwers framed as "one asshole" per Violent J and "on the people who threw rocks".
- FBI gang listing / lawsuits / 2017 march: left out entirely.
- Deaths and overdoses: one short straight beat at the end of CAVE-IN-ROCK.
- Drugs: only the overdose count; Village Voice "Drug Bridge" and the 2026 Missouri drug seizure left out.
- No lyric quotes: "Miracles" paraphrased (giraffes, rainbows, water/fire/air/dirt, magnets, no scientists).
- Religion angle (Ronson/Guardian 2010 "secretly Christian" story): left out (polarizing-adjacent, not needed).
- Tila Tequila's later controversies: left out. She is not a suspect; she's the one who got hurt.
- Interview profanity trimmed; kept "bullshit our way into Peoria", "don't give a fuck about the controversy", "get the fuck out of there" (contract rule, load-bearing), "one asshole".

## Origins
- Joseph Bruce (Violent J) and Joseph Utsler (Shaggy 2 Dope) met in the Detroit area (Oak Park), backyard wrestling; Inner City Posse; late 1991 overspent, switched to horrorcore; Utsler suggested keeping the I.C.P. initials; Bruce's dream of a clown running around Delray; Joker's Cards / Dark Carnival; Psychopathic Records 1991 with Alex Abbiss. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insane_Clown_Posse
- Blender (Sept 2003) voted ICP "the worst band of any musical genre". Same.
- Faygo: Violent J to The Stranger (Kelly O, May 22, 2013): "We always drank Faygo." ... "Faygo Cola was only 69 cents for a two-liter." https://www.thestranger.com/music/kelly-o-gets-down-with-the-clown-16834549/
- 1993 Faygo bottle at hecklers giving them the finger: Wikipedia credits Utsler in one place, Dallas Observer and others say Violent J. Script says "one of the clowns", framed "the story goes". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insane_Clown_Posse ; https://www.dallasobserver.com/music/four-reasons-why-an-insane-clown-posse-show-isnt-so-bad-6426221
- The Great Milenko: Hollywood Records (Disney) recalled it within hours of release, June 24, 1997, after 18,000 copies sold (No. 63 Billboard 200); Disney said it "did not fit the Disney image"; Island re-released it, platinum. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_Milenko ; https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/june-24/disney-pulls-album-on-release-day ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insane_Clown_Posse

## First Gathering, Novi Expo Center, July 21-22, 2000
- Violent J to Spin (Christopher R. Weingarten, "Crazy Clown Time", Aug 2012): "To be totally honest, my brother put it together." Shaggy: "We had a dunk tank where you could dunk a hillbilly." "The first one, we really didn't have no idea what we were doing." Violent J: "They're a convention center, they're used to collectible baseball cards or something." Ring collapse under ~200 fans. https://www.spinmagazine.com/2012/08/crazy-clown-time-history-gathering-juggalos/
- Rob Bruce = Jumpsteady, Violent J's brother. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gathering_of_the_Juggalos
- Sonicnet, Adam Graham, July 25, 2000 ("Insane Clown Posse Fans Rush Stage At Convention"): 7,000+ fans (Wikipedia says ~2,000; script avoids the number); admission $60; ~300 fans onstage; show ended abruptly at 10:40 p.m.; two small parking lot fires; no arrests; "Jesse Satterfield, 25, of Los Angeles, traveled 53 hours with seven of his friends by bus to attend the Gathering."; Bill Syckle "paid $1,750 for a pair of all-access passes"; Violent J: "The money we're spending on the Gathering Of The Juggalos, we could be spending on some gigantic video,"; next year tentatively planned for Chicago. https://riddlebox.be/homepage/gathering/2000/the_gathering_2000_article.htm
- Jugga-dad: Alex Christodoulou (12 in 2000) to the Detroit News (July 21, 2020 oral history), via A.V. Club (Reid McCarter, July 22, 2020): "My dad, for the most part, dropped me off and he hung out in the car." / "So he would show up every hour or two and he would have the whole car filled with Faygo." / "And he would bring cheeseburgers for people and hand out a bunch of food." / "So he was the first official Jugga-dad, so to speak." https://www.avclub.com/faygo-spraying-fan-wistfully-recalls-the-first-gatherin-1844467806

## 2001 to 2006 (Spin 2012 oral history, pages 2 to 4, plus Wikipedia)
- Toledo 2001, SeaGate Convention Centre: stage rush, ended early (Wikipedia). Shaggy: hotel and convention center trashed; "somebody punched a police horse in the face or something like that." Violent J: "We got kicked out of Toledo. Banned from Toledo. We didn't even play Toledo for ten years after that." https://www.spin.com/2012/08/crazy-clown-time-history-gathering-juggalos/2/
- Shaggy: "Basically, the convention centers all know each other" ; "We had to fucking bullshit our way into Peoria, saying that we'd banned all stickers and shit." Same page.
- Peoria 2002: tear gas and pepper balls; Bubba Sparxxx booed off, later "Bubba Sparxxx award". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gathering_of_the_Juggalos
- 2003 Nelson Ledges Quarry Park, Garrettsville, Ohio, first outdoor, 24 hours: Violent J "It was like the key fit in the lock, finally." https://www.spin.com/2012/08/crazy-clown-time-history-gathering-juggalos/3/
- 2004 briefing acts: "woop woop" sounds like "boo" (paraphrased in script); Shaggy: "We always put it in their contracts, too." "If they feel they're in any kind of danger, get the fuck out of there." Same page.
- 2005 quarry owner Evan Kelley removed them; 2006 Michigan International Speedway permits denied by Woodstock Township; moved to Frontier Ranch, Pataskala, Ohio. Violent J: "And they kicked us out two weeks before." "That was the only year in my opinion that has a black eye on it." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gathering_of_the_Juggalos ; https://www.spinmagazine.com/2012/08/crazy-clown-time-history-gathering-juggalos/4/

## Cave-In-Rock / Hogrock, 2007 to 2013
- Shaggy: "After the year before, it was just such a relief." "And it's private grounds, not even police allowed on it for shit." Violent J: "They were so good to us, they were like, we don't give a fuck about the controversy, you guys are the shit." https://www.spinmagazine.com/2012/08/crazy-clown-time-history-gathering-juggalos/4/
- Hogrock owner Tim "Hog Daddy" York; hosts the Hogrock River Rally (biker rally) every year since 1996. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hogrock_Ranch_%26_Campground
- Vice, Thomas Morton, Sept 30, 2007: town ~350 people; last ferry across the Ohio; minister's ban campaign "ended up doing little more than providing fodder for the local paper"; message board "a hilarious shouting match between residents"; two restaurants closed for the dates; schedule 1 PM to 6 AM with wrestling, autographs, movies, magic show, talent show, poker, carnival rides, helicopter rides. https://www.vice.com/en/article/land-of-juggalos-v14n10/
- Pollstar, Aug 8, 2008: shopkeeper to the Southern Illinoisan: "They were nice kids from all over the world," "They spent a lot of money here, and we can use all the business we can get." https://news.pollstar.com/2008/08/08/a-gang-of-juggalos/
- River pirates: Samuel Mason's tavern in the cave around 1800; How the West Was Won (1962) shot partly there. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cave-in-Rock_State_Park ; https://www.wkms.org/post/samuel-mason-cave-rock-pirate-who-prowled-regions-waterways
- "About six hours south of Chicago": driving estimate (~330 miles), Claude's own map check, not from a source.
- 2009: 20,000+, biggest ever, Ice Cube, Scarface; Violent J: "It was the 10th, so we went farther and booked more bands than we ever had." SNL parodied the infomercial ("Kickspit Underground Rock Festival"). https://www.spinmagazine.com/2012/08/crazy-clown-time-history-gathering-juggalos/5/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gathering_of_the_Juggalos ; https://www.dallasobserver.com/opinion/the-gathering-of-the-juggalos-infomercials-ranked-7069470/
- Village Voice 2010: Faygo "by the truckload"; Faygo sno-cones; dunk tank with "a mean clown, of course". https://www.villagevoice.com/2010/09/08/live-from-insane-clown-posses-gathering-of-the-juggalos/
- Serious beat: WKMS, Aug 9, 2013: Deputy Bobby Deppin reported "10 overdoses and 5 drug-related arrests" in the first two full days. AP via WKMS, Dec 3, 2013: one man died during that summer's festival at Hogrock. https://www.wkms.org/arts-culture/2013-08-09/the-annual-gathering-of-the-juggalos-has-illinois-police-on-alert ; https://www.wkms.org/arts-culture/2013-12-03/juggalos-moving-from-cave-in-rock-sheriff-cites-vendor-dispute
