## Cold open (about 30 sec)

*(PA announcer voice, flat and bored)*

"Ladies and gentlemen. Due to unforeseen circumstances, tonight's performance has been cancelled. Refunds will not be issued. Please exit in an orderly fashion."

*(Normal voice)*

Every great music disaster ends with a sentence like that. Somebody in a suit types it up, puts it on the venue door, and goes home.

This show is about everything that happened *before* that sentence got typed.

Hey. Pull up a stool. I'm Dillon, and this is Defacing the Music.

## What the show is (about 2 min)

Here's the deal. Every episode, I take one real night from music history where everything went sideways. Fires. Riots. Arrests. A guy biting something he really should not have bitten. I tell you the whole story, start to finish, like we're sitting at the bar and I just said "wait, you've never heard about this?"

Think of me as that one teacher you had who would derail the lesson plan to tell you something insane that actually happened, and you learned more in those ten minutes than the whole semester. That's the energy. Except this classroom has a two drink minimum.

The name. "Facing the music" means you finally deal with the consequences of what you did. And here's your first fun fact: nobody's totally sure where the phrase comes from. There are a couple of theories, and one involves nervous stage performers literally having to face the orchestra pit. I like that one, because it means the phrase was born from somebody about to bomb on stage.

`[RIFF: if you know another theory you like, toss it in. Otherwise just "anyway, pick your favorite, they're both probably wrong."]`

So: *Defacing* the Music. These are the people who were supposed to face the music, and instead spray-painted a mustache on it and set it on fire.

And every episode title reads like one of those cancellation notices. "Due To" whatever happened. Then a "Who Let" line, because somebody always let it happen. There's always a guy. We're going to find that guy.

## House rules (about 1.5 min)

Couple house rules, then we're done with the syllabus.

**One. Fifteen minutes.** That's it. One story, one beer, out the door. Commute length. Dog walk length.

**Two. It's all real.** The whole point of this show is stuff so dumb it sounds made up, and it isn't. So if a detail is shaky, if it's one of those things everybody repeats but nobody can prove, I'm going to tell you. You'll hear me say "the legend goes" or "the story goes." That's my way of saying "I'm passing this along, but I wasn't there and neither was the guy who told me."

`[RIFF: something about how the internet is full of fake music facts. Optional.]`

**Three. We punch up.** Some of these nights, real people got hurt. When that happens, I'll tell you straight and we won't make the jokes about them. The jokes are for the promoters charging four bucks for water, the label guys signing the check, and the rock star who thought, "you know what this needs? More."

So what's on the setlist?

Keith Moon's bass drum is rigged with a little flash powder for a big finish, and somebody decides a little isn't enough. It goes off, it singes Pete Townshend's hair, and Pete has blamed that night for part of his hearing damage ever since.

`[RIFF: one line about Keith Moon as a person. Your "how do I get this 5-star hotel room to look like a 1-star" line goes here or in ep 1, your call.]`

**Episode two.** Woodstock '99. Peace, love, and a four dollar bottle of water on a concrete Air Force base in July. Personally relevant, since my band plays Limp Bizkit songs for a living, and Limp Bizkit played Woodstock '99. By the last night there were fires, and the Red Hot Chili Peppers were on stage playing a Jimi Hendrix song called "Fire." That's real. I couldn't write that.

**Episode three.** Des Moines, Iowa, 1982. A kid throws a bat on stage at an Ozzy Osbourne show. Ozzy thinks it's a rubber toy. It is not a rubber toy. And here's the part most people don't know: that wasn't even Ozzy's first time biting the head off an animal in public. But that's a story for the episode.

And after that? Teaser round:
